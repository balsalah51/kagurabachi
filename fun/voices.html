Cypic series, announced

Taihi Kimura as Chihiro Rokuhira. The boy who talks to goldfish and will not spend innocents. A new lead on a book that already has a meme history; the job is to make chapter 1’s counter and chapter 14’s True Realm sound like the same nervous system.

Tomokazu Seki as Kunishige Rokuhira. The smith. Impossible eyes. A voice a lot of English viewers already have a shelf for. The war book will ask him to be a picky dealer who barely eats before it asks him to be a ghost in a cellar.

Katsuyuki Konishi as Togo Shiba. Teleport, mouth, extraction. The uncle in the booth. If the adaptation understands the book, Shiba is funny because he is tired, not because he is a mascot.
