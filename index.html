Hokazono has said a revenge story with clothes stained black by blood wants white, black, and red. Volume 1’s Jump Comics jacket is rusty ruby asphalt, a white title lockup, and the black goldfish of Kuro. Gold enters as Enten’s fish — Aka, Kuro, Nishiki — the household pets that became a fighting language.
