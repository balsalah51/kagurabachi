Chihiro and Azamiチヒロと薊

The friend who stayed inside meets the son who would not stay out of the city.

The warning

Azami stayed in the Kamunabi when Shiba left. He warned Chihiro off Sojo. The first book proves the warning and ignores it. Chihiro stays in the Tokyo underworld. The Anti-Cloud Gouger Special Forces die doing the state’s version. Enten bisects the weather sword anyway.

The Rakuzaichi deal is Magatsumi to the state, Enten in the son’s hand, Chihiro on the books. Azami is the kind of adult that deal imagines. Kasen is the director the deal actually gets. The son is already past the warning.

The late state

Headquarters week spends Kudo and loses the Sword Master. Azami’s capital is the building’s last argument that the bureau is not only a leak. Chihiro’s goldfish are still a household. See Azami and Kamunabi.
