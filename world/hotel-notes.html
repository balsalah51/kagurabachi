Already on the page

The Kyoto Bloodshed Hotel is Yojiro Sengoku’s house: Reigen One-Sword Style, a staff trained as a school, a building the Masumi pick as a veil when Samura’s Owl is up and the Hishaku want Iori. It is not a gag name the translation invented for flavor. Bloodshed is the house’s business and then the week’s weather. Chapters 67 through 74 live here. Volume 8’s jacket is the silhouette under Owl: Chihiro, Iori, Hiruhiko.

Sengoku dies so Toto can read a head. That sentence is the hotel’s ethics in one cut. A swordsman who taught a style becomes a sample in a tracker’s book. Kuguri, sent to close the kidnapping, stays because Chihiro’s fake Iai is not fake enough to ignore. Hiruhiko, already beaten once on the train, already contracted to Kumeyuri, wrecks the upper floors with Play because he does not respect objects. Samura arrives because two Enchanted Blades in one hotel is a beacon a nationwide owl can see. Feathers, banquet, goldfish. The daughter and the father occupy the same city again.
