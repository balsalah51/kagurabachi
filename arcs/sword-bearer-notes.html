Already on the page

The deal with the Kamunabi has a first assignment. A Sanso is attacked. Chihiro and Hakuri are sent to keep Yoji Uruha alive. Uruha was chosen young and never recovered from being chosen. Kunishige’s death emptied him; Chihiro’s existence refills the oath. The Hishaku send Hiruhiko and Datenseki infantry at the train to Kokugoku. Blood Crane boards. The fight falls into a kabuki house because Hiruhiko is the one sent to make a scene.

Hakuri’s Storehouse is why the assignment can work at all. He can register a bearer and a blade and move steel without a siege. At Senkutsuji he returns Tobimune to Seiichi Samura. Samura clears the temple at a speed the rest of the bearers treat as weather, then cuts Uruha down. It looks like a Hishaku pact. Guilt over the Malediction put Samura in a room with Yura: kill the other bearers, return the blades, kill the Sword Master, then die with the ten. What he actually does to Uruha is Suzaku. Kill the Lifelong Contract. Keep the man. Kumeyuri comes off the board. Uruha wakes in a morgue with Crimson Recital limping back.
