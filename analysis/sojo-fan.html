Sojo, Kunishige’s worst fan最悪のファン

He understood the craft as slaughter and called that honesty. Chihiro understood the house.

TV Tropes files Sojo as a loony fan and it is not wrong. It is incomplete. He is the only early villain who can talk about the Enchanted Blades without sounding like a government memo. He wants to mass-produce Kunishige. He wants Char’s clan as a stabilizer. He wants Cloud Gouger to “gain slaughter” and is delighted when it does. That last wish is True Realm as autobiography.

Chihiro hates him and recognizes him. Both of them think the smith’s work is the most important object in Japan. Sojo never had to live with the man. He never saw the bowl, the picky dealer, the cellar of swords that would not break. Fandom without the household becomes a factory. The bathhouse extras are funny because the curiosity is the same organ as the cruelty.

Every later monster is a larger Sojo argument. Yura is the collector. Akemura is the patriot who finished the thought. Hiruhiko is the child who thinks friendship is a stage. The book keeps asking: if you love the blades, who are you loyal to — the steel, or the person who was sorry he made it?
