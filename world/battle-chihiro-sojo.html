Hokazono has named this the favorite fight he has drawn so far.

The street

Chapter 9 titles the match: Enten vs. Cloud Gouger (淵天vs刳雲). Volume 2’s jacket is this fight. Sojo is a customer, not Hishaku. Ibuki already held the weather sword in the war. Hokuto already opened the contract. Yura already sold the steel in early October.

Mei, cloaked Mei, Yui, Kou. Chapter 10 is Swift. Chapter 11 is Awaken. Chapter 14 is True Realm: Sojo’s Cloud Gouger gains slaughter. Chapter 16 is Silence. Chapter 17 is Tea. Chapter 18 is Roar. Enten bisects the sword. Sojo dies in the Datenseki backlash of a mineral he could not tame.

The state’s version

The Anti-Cloud Gouger Special Forces die in the same city: Hagiwara, Harima, Uzuki, Kasahara, Kugara, Kazane. Six specialists, four dead, a commander who loses both legs. Flame Bone is not in this first weather week. Hiyuki arrives as the next book’s pointed end.

Char stays off the table. Chihiro keeps leftover Cloud Gouger pieces and later spends residual charges at the Rakuzaichi. Notes toward forging the weather sword again sit in the smith’s ledger. See Vs. Sojo and the worst fan.
