A prophetess. A Sword Master. Same promotion.

The lower branch that moved

Wikipedia’s Soga file: Chiaki and her younger brother Akemura are elevated when her ability manifests after the main-branch predecessor dies childless. Foresight is the clan’s female warrant. Magatsumi is the wartime steel the brother contracted.

Hiroto headed the clan and died on Irishima. The sister’s title is Princess. The brother’s press name is Sword Master. Neither is a Jump invention of this desk. See Chiaki, Akemura, Princess.
