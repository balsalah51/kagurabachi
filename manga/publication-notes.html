Kagurabachi is written and illustrated by Takeru Hokazono. It began in Weekly Shōnen Jump issue 2023 #42, on 19 September 2023. Jump Comics Volume 1, Mission, landed 2 February 2024 (ISBN 978-4-08-883819-9). As of 4 September 2026 there are twelve Japanese tankōbon. Volume 12 released 4 September 2026 (ISBN 978-4-08-885177-8). VIZ Media and MANGA Plus carry the English simulpub the same week as the magazine. VIZ’s first print volume arrived 5 November 2024 (ISBN 978-1-9747-4724-5). English Volume 8 is out; Volume 9 is listed for 3 November 2026; Volume 10 is listed for 2 February 2027 (ISBN 978-1-9747-6947-6). This page is the circulation and awards record. The volume guide is the ISBN table. The chapter index is the title list through 132, “Misaka.”

Promo art was a black coat, white type, and a fish. English Twitter decided it was the next Big Three before page one existed. Chapter 1 was the most-viewed new title on MANGA Plus in its first week.
