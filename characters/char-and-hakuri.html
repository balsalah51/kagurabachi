A child the clan warehoused. A son the clan disowned.

Two leftovers of the 208th

The Rakuzaichi warehouses people. Char is the Kyonagi girl Sojo also wanted. Hakuri is the Sazanami son already decided leftover. Chihiro walks into that house for both jobs: a child out, a masterpiece not sold, a seventh blade still in his hand.

Hakuri becomes the Storehouse. Char becomes a cafe-adjacent life the later book still has to protect. Miyari Nemoto is Char’s anime voice. See Char, Hakuri, cargo.
