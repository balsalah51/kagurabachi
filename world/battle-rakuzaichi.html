The listing

The Sazanami house is the building. The Storehouse is the actual building: a subspace. Chihiro lets them take Enten so the blade can scout the Kura on its own charge. On auction day he walks back in with Cloud Gouger’s stump. Chapter 19 is Knight of Darkness. Chapter 23 is Storehouse. Chapter 27 is Mr. Inazuma.

Hakuri wakes Isou, then the Storehouse. He is the only living Sazanami to hold both. Soya is the heir he has to fight. Tenri eats a half-stable Datenseki tool and pops. Hiyuki’s Flame Bone meets Kyora’s warehouse. Prisoners leave. The building has to live long enough for that.

The look

Kyora, dying, looks through Shinuchi and sees the Sword Master. The 208th listed a masterpiece. The eleventh head dies having seen the man the masterpiece is contracted to. Chihiro hands Magatsumi to the Kamunabi and keeps Enten by joining them. The firm ends. Volume 5’s closer is Unruly Punk. The next title is Uruha.
