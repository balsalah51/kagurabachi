Reception

Meme to flagshipネタから本誌へ

People decided a guy with a sword was the next Big Three before page one existed. Then the book had to survive being liked for real.

September 2023. Promo art: black coat, white type, three goldfish. English Twitter invented a career for it. “The new Big Three.” “Generational.” Irony so thick it became a distribution strategy. Chapter 1 was the most-viewed new title on MANGA Plus in its first week. By April 2024 the app had logged over 99 million page views. It beat Spy × Family, Dragon Ball Super, and Boruto on the global ranking. People were actually reading.

Jump 2023 #42, 19 September. First long work after a Tezuka one-shot already named Enten and a run of Giga / Jump shorts. VIZ and MANGA Plus ran the English simulpub the same week, which is why a hashtag in New York could become a first-week chart in every timezone the app serves. Chapter 1 is usually free forever on MANGA Plus. That is how the joke became readers. People did not have to find a scan. They had to tap a title.

Hokazono walked into an Anime NYC room that was not billed as a main event and found it stuffed. ANN’s write-up is the useful one: the joke fandom became a real one because the pages were good, not because the hashtag was loud. He talked goldfish, John Wick streets, and a favorite fight, Chihiro vs Sojo, in a room that had come for a meme and stayed for a craftsman. Next Manga Award, print category, 2024. Nominations later for Shogakukan, Kodansha, and an Eisner international slot. Highest-voted title in a Manga Plus AX poll over Sakamoto Days, RuriDragon, and Blue Box.

Circulation: 1 million by October 2024, 2.2 million by May 2025, 3 million by October 2025, 4 million by April 2026. Eleven Japanese volumes by May 2026; volume 12 listed for 4 September. VIZ’s English tankōbon started 5 November 2024 and has eight volumes released or solicited through late 2026. An anime from Cypic, directed by Tetsuya Takeuchi, is scheduled for April 2027. The teaser visual still sells the three fish. Official tags now include #BachiAnime. The 2023 posts did not plan that. The pages did.

The archive’s position is unromantic. Ironic hype got the first chapter opened. The Vs. Sojo fight, Hakuri, and Samura are why people stayed. Volume 2 is the duel. Volumes 3–5 are the auction. Volumes 6–11 are the long book that turns a revenge file into a retraction. If you are sending the book to someone who only knows the meme, send the first-read notes and chapter 1 on MANGA Plus. The pitch video people still send lives on the theories page under mystery-as-engine. Pair it with this page so the word “generational” has a circulation number next to it.

The word bachi in the title is still a Sunday in-joke, punishment, a strike, a sound. Kagura is shrine dance. The title is the book’s joke and its method. The teaser sold a goldfish. He told you why.
