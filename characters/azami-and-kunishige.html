The split

After the Malediction, Kunishige confiscates the six swords and hides with Azami and Shiba. Shiba leaves the Kamunabi. Azami stays inside. That is not a loyalty score. It is two ways to keep a smith alive in a state that will later leak his address.

Azami warns Chihiro off Sojo. The pointed end of the bureau is Hiyuki. The leak is Kasen. Azami is the director-shaped adult who wanted blades sealed and still could not stop a mailing. Volume 11’s jacket argument includes a man spending himself against Magatsumi while the leak is already stationery.
