Bearer · Enten

Chihiro Rokuhira

Son of the swordsmith. Eighteen. The seventh blade was made with him, and for a reason that is not revenge.
Storehouse

Hakuri Sazanami

Disowned auction heir who unlocked both Isou and the clan subspace — historically almost unheard of.
Tobimune

Seiichi Samura

Fastest of the war bearers. Blind by choice. Iai White Purity Style, and a father who tried to disappear.
Sword Master

Akemura Soga

Bearer of Magatsumi. Chihiro’s uncle. The man the history books called a hero after the island burned.

Genichi Sojo

The first rival who loved the smith’s work. Every later monster is a larger version of this argument.
