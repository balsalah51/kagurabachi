Already on the page

A Sword Bearer (Shoyūsha) is anyone on a Lifelong Contract. The steel takes the innate art and leaves weather. The Kamunabi locked the wartime survivors in Sanso after the raid and called that safety. The Hishaku treated the same list as a kill order. Enten sits outside the wartime six; Chihiro is still a bearer. This roster follows the printed pages. Two wartime blades still have no titles. Subaru is one surviving name. The other portrait in the war ensemble is still blank.

The master key is Magatsumi. If Akemura dies, the other five wartime bearers die with him. Destroy Magatsumi and the knot comes apart. Cut the contract without cutting the person, Samura’s Suzaku, and the old art limps back. Those three sentences are the whole politics of Part 1. Contracts: Lifelong Contracts. Steel: Enchanted Blades.
