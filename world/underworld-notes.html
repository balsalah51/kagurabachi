Already on the page

After the raid, Chihiro and Shiba do not join the Kamunabi. They go into Tokyo’s sorcerer underworld and stay there until the auction forces a different building. Small jobs. Hinao’s lights. A sighting of Char. Madoka talking. Sojo’s compound. The Anti-Cloud Gouger squad dying in the same city trying to do the state’s version of the work. The Kamunabi want Chihiro out of the street. He stays. Location file: Tokyo. Cafe: Haru Haru.

A modern Japan that had to admit sorcery in public, then lie about what it did with it. Civilian sorcerers handle small jobs: debts, retrievals, the kind of work that does not need a national stamp. The Kamunabi exist because some jobs are national. Enchanted Blades are the rare version of Datenseki that overflow as weather instead of craters. Most people on this street will never see one. Chihiro walks in with the seventh and a coat already stained. That is why the early chapters look like a revenge story about a boy with a secret sword. They are a job board that keeps being interrupted by a national weapon.
