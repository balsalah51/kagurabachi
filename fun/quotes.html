Not a merch shirt list. The sentences the book actually taught the room to say, with the stills they belong to.

Sunday quotes a panel, then argues about the translation. This page keeps the ones that survived being memed: Hokazono on fins, VIZ on True Realm, Hakuri calling himself empty, Enten as a retraction. Pictures first. The essays are linked where the line is doing work. Official chapters remain on VIZ and MANGA Plus. We paraphrase where a verbatim balloon would be a scan.

“I really intended to go with koi until the last minute.”

“In a revenge tale where the protagonist’s clothes are stained black with blood, the colors white, black, and red match well.”
Same interview. Volume 1’s jacket is that sentence printed. Later books invite one extra hue when a rival blade is on stage.
