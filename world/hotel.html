Kyoto Bloodshed Hotel血戦ホテル

The Masumi’s chosen dark. Chihiro learns to draw with his eyes shut. Iori remembers a father. Hiruhiko takes the upper floors apart.

The Kyoto Bloodshed Hotel is Yojiro Sengoku’s house: Reigen One-Sword Style, a staff trained as a school, a building the Masumi pick as a veil when Samura’s Owl is up and the Hishaku want Iori. It is not a gag name the translation invented for flavor. Bloodshed is the house’s business and then the week’s weather. Chapters 67 through 74 live here. Volume 8’s jacket is the silhouette under Owl: Chihiro, Iori, Hiruhiko.

Sengoku dies so Toto can read a head. That sentence is the hotel’s ethics in one cut. A swordsman who taught a style becomes a sample in a tracker’s book. Kuguri, sent to close the kidnapping, stays because Chihiro’s fake Iai is not fake enough to ignore. Hiruhiko, already beaten once on the train, already contracted to Kumeyuri, wrecks the upper floors with Play because he does not respect objects. Samura arrives because two Enchanted Blades in one hotel is a beacon a nationwide owl can see. Feathers, banquet, goldfish. The daughter and the father occupy the same city again.

The classroom

Chihiro copies by sight. Iai White Purity requires the lids down. The hotel is where those two facts meet a live instructor who did not volunteer. Kuguri scorns hobby swordsmanship. A boy shutting his eyes in a corridor is the opposite of a hobby once the cuts start landing. Chapter 65 is “Imitate.” Chapter 70 is “Iai White Purity Style.” Those titles are this building even when the table of contents does not print the address on them. Itsuo Shirakai founded the school in the mountains and did not want women in it. Iori’s body already knows the curriculum under a seal. Chihiro learns it in public. Kuguri is the unwilling adjunct.

Toto holds the clock. Kuguri drops the errand. The split is the ten: one member falls in love with the fight, one member still remembers they came for a girl. Sumi’s motorcycle is the road in. Sengoku’s Reigen is the building. Chihiro’s decision to tell Iori the truth rather than re-seal it is the reason the veil is also a confession. Ikura, classmate, loner, trails Toto and is part of why the seal finally breaks. Iori shields him. Memory returns as protection, not as a lecture.

The demolition

Kumeyuri’s Play manipulates nearby objects. Fluency scales with respect. Hiruhiko’s extension does not respect objects. Destructive Play is an asterisk on a hotel coming apart. Banquet is the other technique: intoxicating hallucinations, mitigated by reinforced ears, snapped by a fatal wound. Samura walks into both. Tobimune versus a banquet sword in a building that used to be a school. Ro of the Masumi later recovers Kumeyuri after Samura takes it off Hiruhiko. A ninja clan’s leader doing inventory on a banquet blade is this book’s idea of logistics.

Toto extracts who she can through the fire-gate when the scene turns fatal. Kuguri is pulled out rather than finished. The kidnapping fails. Iori’s seal is already broken. The hotel remains a ruin. Volume 8 is titled Dawn. Dawn after a night battle is a silhouette and a lot of dust.

What the building was

Reigen One-Sword Style is a house style, not Iai and not an Enchanted Blade school. Sengoku taught the staff. The Masumi chose the dark because a swordsman’s hotel can hide a girl if the tracker has not already read a sample. Toto had Samura’s blood from Senkutsuji. The dark was never complete. Easy Does It, the operation name, is the book being funny about a week that includes a beheading, a demolished upper floor, and a father arriving on owl-light.
