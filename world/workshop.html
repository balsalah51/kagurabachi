Setting

The Rokuhira workshop六平の工房

A house over a cellar of swords that would not break. A bowl on the table. Then three Hishaku at the door.

Kunishige hid after the Seitei War with Azami and Shiba and raised Chihiro over a failure he could not smash. Six wartime Enchanted Blades in the cellar. Every attempt to destroy them failed. The seventh, Enten, is the house’s answer: a counter-blade forged with the son as a teenager, goldfish because the daily life between them needed a household object, not a carp. The workshop is not a prologue set. It is the book’s first moral geometry. A picky dealer who barely eats. A bowl the fish like more depending on who feeds them. A cellar that will not yield.

Rooms

The shop floor. Ore, heat, the eyes that made Datenseki safe once. Hokazono talked to a real swordsmith so later smelting would not be cosplay. Part 2’s kiln on Irishima is this room’s homework coming due. Chihiro grew up learning to look at metal the way his father did. The stoicism on Sunday is workshop manners.

The table. Three goldfish. Kuro, Aka, Nishiki named for the household, not a battlefield. Chapter 1 will not circle the bowl. It assumes you can watch furniture become a moveset and still remember it was furniture. See the bowl and the fins interview.

The cellar. Six swords that would not break on an anvil. The Kamunabi wanted them sealed. Kunishige wanted them gone. Enten exists because neither wish fit in the same lock. When the Hishaku raid the house, they take the six and miss the seventh. That is not luck. That is a father who built the spare for the son, not for the state.

The raid

Three years before the present tense. Three Hishaku sorcerers. Kunishige is killed in front of Chihiro. The six wartime blades leave. Enten stays. Uran is already in the workshop that night; Chihiro’s later commute is three years away from her breath and still inside it. Kasen’s leak is why the address was known. The house stops being a home and becomes a crime scene the son will not stop walking back into, even when the walking is Tokyo underworld, an auction, a hotel, a train to Irishima.

After the house

Cafe Haru Haru is the civilian door Shiba uses when the coat is too loud. The Kamunabi want the remaining blades in Sanso boxes. Chihiro works the underworld with Shiba, then hands Magatsumi to the bureau and keeps Enten by joining them. That is the smith’s son treating the state as a tool the way the state treated his father. By the end of Part 1 he is taking notes for a new Enten, and even a new Cloud Gouger, from the pieces he still holds. The workshop is back in his head before it is back on Irishima as a kiln.

Part 2 returns to the mineral the house was always about. Chiaki Soga, the mother, a princess with foresight. Kunishige before he was a hermit: a picky weapons dealer who had not yet looked at the ore. The cellar was a consequence. The bowl was a choice. The raid made a revenge story. The workshop is why the revenge story keeps naming the father.
