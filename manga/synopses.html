The volume guide is the page for dates, ISBNs, chapter maps, and jacket faces. This page is the story those spines are holding, written long enough that a reader who has the books can argue with it and a reader who does not can still feel the commute. Chapter ranges for middle volumes are compiled from public jacket copy and wiki concordances. If a collected edition shifts a chapter, the chapter index is the authority. Official chapters: VIZ / MANGA Plus. We do not host them. Volume extras live in the tankōbon; see bathhouse and extras.

Japanese Jump Comics run about nine months ahead of VIZ. Eleven volumes as of May 2026. Volume 12 is solicited for 4 September 2026 (ISBN 978-4-08-885177-8) and is expected to close Part 1 and open the war book. Palette notes belong to the cover studies. What follows is not a wiki plot dump. It is this site talking through each book the way the jackets already started the sentence.

Volume 1: Mission すべきこと

Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

2 February 2024. ISBN 978-4-08-883819-9. Chapters 1–8. Jacket: Chihiro. Palette: rusty ruby, asphalt red, white title, Kuro goldfish. The law of the series in one object.

Three years after the raid, Chihiro Rokuhira takes Enten into Tokyo. The workshop is a grave he still works from. Togo Shiba is the extraction adult. Hinao’s Cafe Haru Haru is the sit-down between jobs, and the sit-down is also a labor market: sorcerers to yakuza and corporations. Char Kyonagi, last of a regenerative clan, says she has seen an Enchanted Blade. Norisaku Madoka, Daruma sorcerer on Sojo’s payroll, confirms the weather and then decides to go straight. Chapter 8 is his name and a promise. Sojo kills him for talking. Azami, still inside the Kamunabi, wants the boy away from the customer who just bought a stolen sword. The bowl is already on the table. The reader does not yet have the word True Realm. They have a mission, heaps, a witness, tea later. The Hishaku are not yet a photograph. They are the reason a teenager is waiting tables with a goldfish sword.

English: 5 November 2024, ISBN 978-1-9747-4724-5. This is the book the 2023 meme had to survive. Chapter 1 is usually free forever on MANGA Plus. Send that, not a compilation.

Volume 2: Enten vs. Cloud Gouger 淵天 VS 刳雲

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

2 May 2024. ISBN 978-4-08-883880-9. Chapters 9–18. Jacket: Chihiro, Sojo. Palette: night steel versus storm cyan; the first extra hue on a red-black book.

The hospital. The Anti-Cloud Gouger Special Forces: six people built to solve one sword. Sojo treats them as weather treats a forecast. Four graves. Hagiwara loses both legs. Kazane loses an arm before Kaichi is the scene. Chihiro loses an arm and finds the True Realm: the brief, meant, not a transformation gauge. Cloud Gouger breaks. Mei, Yui, Kou, then black lightning when the blade is dying. Sojo chooses Datenseki over a quiet death because he loved Kunishige’s work and never met the household. He tries to fake the eyes with Kyonagi flesh and pops himself the way Tenri will later pop on a smaller stone. Hokazono has named this fight as his favorite so far. The volume extra will later let the corpse rate tubs. The magazine chapters let him die of the mineral.

Without Hokuto’s off-page kill of Ibuki, this book has no weather sword to sell. Recaps that start Sojo as a Hishaku member cannot explain a customer. Yura sold him a blade. Volume 2 is the receipt.

Volume 3: Knight of Darkness 闇の騎士

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

4 July 2024. ISBN 978-4-08-884116-8. Chapters 19–27. Jacket: Chihiro, Hiyuki, Kyora. Palette: flame-orange against auction green-black.

Hiyuki Kagari arrives, Flame Bone of the Starving, the Kamunabi’s pointed end, Tafuku’s duel-domain partner somewhere in the act. Shinuchi is listed at the 208th Rakuzaichi. The Sazanami estate is two centuries of market calling itself civilization. Chihiro surrenders Enten on purpose so the seventh blade can scout the Storehouse on its own charge. A goldfish sword walking a warehouse that thinks people are inventory. Yuu Inazuma wants his sister out. The Tou exist as a family military. Kyora is polite and absolute. Hakuri is still the discarded son in this book, not yet the architecture. The title is Hiyuki’s shadow and Chihiro’s, and also the auction’s: darkness as a knight’s job and as a sales floor.

Volume 4: Equal 対等

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

4 October 2024. ISBN 978-4-08-884209-7. Chapters 28–36. Jacket: Chihiro, Hakuri. Palette: two figures, storehouse dusk. The title is the relationship the clan refused to grant.

Hakuri was scattering his own spirit energy. The family read the scatter as defect. When he chooses Chihiro over the Rakuzaichi, Isou answers. Storehouse opens because he remembered a woman the auction killed, not because Kyora lectured him. He becomes one of two Sazanami in history to hold both. Soya, heir, obsessive, twisted affection as abuse, demands a return, then a kill, then loses to the house. Tenri, desperate to make Kyora proud, uses a half-stable Datenseki tool. Jellyfish, then the pop. Yura walks the auction floor because Magatsumi is his listing; he cracked the seal and left the ropes. Chapter 31’s color page is the auction house starting to answer the discarded son. Equal is not a power level. It is Chihiro refusing to pick a tool up and a boy refusing to be inventory.

Volume 5: Fervent 熱狂

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

4 December 2024. ISBN 978-4-08-884348-3. Chapters 37–46. Jacket: Chihiro, Kyora, Hakuri, Hiyuki. Palette: crowded, Shinuchi heat. The title is the crowd.

Storehouse war. Chihiro spends the last of Cloud Gouger to take Enten back. Kyora unsheathes Magatsumi and almost becomes someone else. The Sword Master looks through an auctioneer’s eyes and still does not get a new bearer. Proxy is not contract. Kyora dies as himself long enough to admit, too late, what he did to Hakuri. Hiyuki and Chihiro keep the building from becoming a second island. Prisoners leave. The Rakuzaichi ends. The clan goes into hiding. Soya crawls out with amnesia. Enji begs Shiba for death and is told to live and raise what is left so another child does not eat a stone. Chihiro hands Magatsumi to the Kamunabi and keeps Enten by joining them. Hakuri is the logistics in that deal: bearers un-armed and re-armed without a siege. The fervor is the crowd’s. The ethics are the two figures from Volume 4, still standing in a building that has started to vanish.

Volume 6: Daybreak 夜更け / 黎明

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

4 March 2025. ISBN 978-4-08-884399-5. Chapters 47–56. Jacket: Uruha, Samura, Chihiro, Hiruhiko. Palette: yin-yang bearers, Chihiro versus Hiruhiko in the blood center.

A Sanso is attacked. The state’s boxes were never going to hold. Train to Kokugoku. Hiruhiko boards: eighteen, killed an assailant at three, Blood Crane, razor origami, a peer-shaped friend Chihiro does not want. Kabuki house. Senkutsuji. The Masumi suppress sound and scent so a blind man can fight by hearing. Hakuri returns Tobimune through Storehouse. Samura clears the temple and then cuts Uruha down. Suzaku: kill the contract, keep the man. It looks like a Hishaku pact. It is a Buddhist room used as a surgery. Uruha’s Crimson Recital will limp back later; in this book he falls. Daybreak is a title that knows the night is not over. The Hishaku think they have a partner who will eliminate bearers. He is lying about the dying.

Volume 7: Night Battle 夜戦

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

2 May 2025. ISBN 978-4-08-884413-8. Chapters 57–65. Jacket: Samura, Chihiro, Iori. Palette: night blue, sunglasses, hotel dark.

The false deaths. Owl over Japan: two giant eyes, nationwide, only huge spirit sources ping. Operation: Easy Does It. The Masumi (Ro, Moku, Sumi) take Iori out of school toward Kyoto. They are the clan that erased her father from her and are now the clan that will not let the ten use her as a lever. Chihiro goes to tell the truth rather than re-seal it. Toto has Samura’s blood from the temple. Kuguri gives chase. Chapter 65 is “Imitate.” The hotel is already a school in the last pages. Iori is still sealed. The night is the point. A support blade that can see the country is also a weather the daughter has to walk under.

Volume 8: Dawn 夜明け

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

4 July 2025. ISBN 978-4-08-884566-1. Chapters 66–74. Jacket: Chihiro, Iori, Hiruhiko. Palette: hotel silhouette under Owl.

Iori’s past. The seal frays because she still loved him and breaks when she shields Ikura, the classmate who trailed Toto. Malediction spoken aloud in a girl’s mouth, not only in an essay. Yojiro Sengoku of the Reigen One-Sword Style dies so Toto can read a head. Kuguri drops the kidnapping because the fake Iai is a real fight. Hiruhiko wrecks the upper floors with Play; he does not respect objects. Samura walks into Banquet and demolition because two Enchanted Blades in one hotel is a ping Owl cannot ignore. Feathers, banquet, goldfish. Dawn is dust. Toto extracts who she can. The kidnapping fails. The daughter occupies the same city as the father again. Ro will later recover Kumeyuri. The hotel remains a ruin. See the hotel.

Volume 9: Enten 淵天

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

3 October 2025. ISBN 978-4-08-884653-8. Chapters 75–86. Jacket: Chihiro alone. Palette: an icon, not a cast photograph. The title and the sword are the same word.

Enten versus Tobimune. Chapter 83 says Kunishige’s brief cleanly: the seventh blade is a retraction, not a seventh trophy. Its True Realm is Magatsumi’s death. Samura opens his eyes. The goldfish are the household made into a fighting language: Kuro, Aka, Nishiki. A single-figure jacket after two ensemble books is a statement. The long book has been about fathers and daughters and hotels. This spine puts the son’s sword on the board as a name you already knew and a purpose you were not yet allowed to spell. The essay lives at what Enten was forged for.

Kasen’s leak is on the table. Headquarters infiltration. Uruha walks again, contract cut, Crimson Recital limping back, the prodigy still loyal to the Rokuhira name. Yukisada is already the lock, even if this jacket does not show the boy. Chapter 91 is “Natsuki.” Chapter 92 is “The Swordsmen.” The book wants you to see blades before factions. Three of the four still think a sword is an argument. One of them thinks it is a government. See the leak as policy.

Volume 11: Heroes 英雄

Previous volume · Next volume · ISBNs · Jacket

1 May 2026. ISBN 978-4-08-885102-0. Chapters 96–105. Jacket: Chihiro, goldfish, snow. Palette: snow white, Aka and Kuro, Enten raised. English TBD.

Yukisada, seventeen, strongest fighter per Yura, regeneration past decapitation, Vessel in the HQ barrier. Hakuri, Kiri, Hagiwara cannot kill him cleanly. Storehouse is the tool that works: remove the Kamunabi vessel from the split. Chapter 98 is “Ikuto Hagiwara, Worthless Commander.” The first book’s cavalry leftover gets a cruel caption and more work. Kudo dies for Hakuri. Bingo’s lion-dancer charms make cleanup expensive. The Sword Master stands up in Yura. The chapter index puts quotation marks on “Heroes” because the press already used the word after the war. This book uses it on a headquarters fight and a snow jacket. Part 1’s last icon before the corridor. The lock on the cell is a teenager. The mind of the ten changes species.
