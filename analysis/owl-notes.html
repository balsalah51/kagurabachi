Owl (梟) is one of Tobimune’s printed techniques. Two giant owl eyes. A detection range versus precision tradeoff. At nationwide scale, only huge spirit sources ping. After Senkutsuji, Samura puts Owl up over Japan. Operation: Easy Does It is the Masumi’s answer: move Iori, because a father who can see the country can also be used as a lever if the daughter is held. The Hishaku understood the lever. Toto had blood. The Kyoto Bloodshed Hotel was the chosen dark. Two Enchanted Blades in one hotel is a beacon. Samura arrives. The technique that was supposed to be a map becomes a door.

Chihiro notes Tobimune was forged for support. Crow swaps places with a feather. Suzaku heals the bearer and later kills contracts while keeping the man. Owl is the support move that makes the whole country a room. A support blade that can see Japan is also a weapon against anyone who needs to hide a girl, a sword, or a lie. Samura’s guilt-religion uses all three functions. He erases himself from Iori, then lights a signal that will drag him back to her the moment someone else draws steel in her city.
