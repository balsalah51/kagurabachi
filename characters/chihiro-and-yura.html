How the pair is printed

Three Hishaku sorcerers hit the workshop. Kunishige dies in front of the son. Six wartime blades leave the cellar. Enten stays. Yura is the man the later book treats as the clan’s will: flame tattoos, a fire-gate, a director who can spend a Kamunabi leak as policy.

Chihiro’s first engine is revenge. Part 2 does not stay only with the son, but the present-tense hunt still has Yura’s name on the other end. Volume 10’s Japanese jacket already groups Yura and Hokuto against Uruha and Natsuki. See Chihiro, Yura, the raid.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
