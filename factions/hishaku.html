The Hishaku毘灼

They killed the smith, stole six blades, and spent three years trying to open contracts by killing the people still tied to them.

The Hishaku are ten elite criminal sorcerers. Flame-emblem tattoos. A shared fire-gate. They formed about four years before the main story with one plan for Shinuchi. Three years before the present, they raided the Rokuhira house, murdered Kunishige, and stole the six wartime Enchanted Blades. Enten stayed with the son. In the same campaign they killed Ibuki Misaka so Cloud Gouger’s Lifelong Contract would open. They are not a gang that happened to find swords. They are a method: open the knot, spend the steel, change the country.

They are allied with Korogumi yakuza and, fatally, with at least one Kamunabi head. Kasen leaked the smith’s address because he thought Enchanted Blades were a path to order. The Hishaku were the path he hired. Recaps that call Sojo Hishaku cannot explain Yura. Sojo is a customer. They handed him Cloud Gouger in early October. He used it to chase Char and the idea that he could fake Kunishige’s eyes. They listed Magatsumi at the 208th Rakuzaichi after Yura cracked the seal and left the ropes. They signed a private contract with Samura. They are the men who sold a sword, auctioned a masterpiece, and tried to kidnap a daughter so a father would flinch.

Eight of the ten are named on this site. This archive will not invent the other two. The named eight are enough to see the labor split: a mind, a swordsman who wants a real fight, a child who killed at three, a tracker, an ice user, a lion-dancer who eats corpses, a seventeen-year-old regenerator in a barrier, and a man courting a blade that will not sign him.

The method

Lifelong Contracts bind a blade to one nervous system. Kill the bearer, the contract opens, someone else can sign. Magatsumi’s contract is the master key: if Akemura dies, the other five wartime bearers die with him. Destroy Magatsumi and the knot comes apart. For three years the Hishaku hit Sanso fortresses trying to open the wartime five that were still alive. Samura’s side deal looked like a shortcut: he would eliminate bearers, return blades, kill the Sword Master, then die with the Hishaku. He was lying about the dying. Suzaku can kill a contract and keep the man. Yura anticipated betrayal anyway and attacked Kamunabi headquarters while other members went after Iori.

The shared fire-gate is logistics. Toto uses it as a habit, pulling comrades out of fatal rooms. The tattoos are the uniform. The plan for Shinuchi mutated. Yura wanted the Sword Master dead so he could wield the masterpiece. Then he spoke to Akemura in the cell, found him sane, and offered a body. The ten did not vote on that. The mind of the set changed the math, and Part 1 ended with the basement wearing the leader.

The named ten (eight printed)

Yura 幽

Leader. Calm enough to bet his life on a coin, sure God is on his side. He ordered the raid, sold Cloud Gouger, cracked Magatsumi’s seal, listed it at the Rakuzaichi, and visited Samura at home to write a contract. He had a wife; she died. He wanted Akemura dead, then wanted Akemura as a colleague. Gravely wounded by Chihiro, he gave the body. Full file: Yura.

Hokuto 北兜

Swordsman in samurai clothes. Armored puppet that resembles himself; piece control for defense. Present at Kunishige’s murder. Assassin of Ibuki. Disappointed that the wartime equal of Samura had already retired. Wants deadly battles, especially with Uruha. Volume 10 jacket with Natsuki, Uruha, and Yura. Full file: Hokuto.

Hiruhiko 昼彦

Eighteen. Killed an assailant at age three. Blood Crane (Chizuru): razor origami, lifting Datenseki fragments. Assigned to kill Uruha, met Chihiro on the train, treated him as a peer-shaped friend. Contracted Kumeyuri after Samura’s false death, two-handed, Play as demolition. Lost the blade to Samura at the hotel. Full file: Hiruhiko.

Kuguri 久々李

Twilight Wave (Hagure): banks kinetic energy and heat, spends it later. Carries an Enchanted Blade without a contract; calls it unrequited love. Sent with Toto to take Iori. Abandoned the kidnapping when Chihiro’s fake Iai got serious. Full file: Kuguri.

Toto 斗斗

Blood tracking and extraction. Locates Iori from a sample taken off Samura at Senkutsuji. At the Kyoto Bloodshed Hotel she reads Sengoku’s severed head the same way. Prefers the fire-gate to the cut. The ten’s extraction specialist. Full file: Toto. Gate desk: fire-gate.

Uran 右嵐

Ice. Breath that freezes and restrains. Present at Kunishige’s murder with Hokuto and the third raider. The raid was three sorcerers. She is one of the three. Full file: Uran.

Bingo 瓶伍

Mako (Demon Bite): lion-dancer heads, “lucky charms,” mouths that hit hard. Destroying them stacks an invisible weight on whoever did the destroying. He can turn his own head into a charm and eat corpses to fuel the art, then gets sleepy. HQ infiltration muscle. Full file: Bingo.

Yukisada 幸禎

Seventeen. Yura calls him the strongest fighter among the Hishaku. Regeneration that survives decapitation. Became a Vessel so he could manipulate Kamunabi headquarters’ barrier from inside, splitting control with the Kamunabi’s own vessel and shutting the building against relief. Hakuri, Kiri, and Hagiwara cannot kill him cleanly. The solution is Storehouse: remove the Kamunabi vessel from the split rather than win a regeneration duel. Yukisada is why Azami cannot simply call Shiba until the field is restored. Volume 11, Heroes, is his lock on the cell. Full file: Yukisada.

What they are not

They are not the Kamunabi. Kasen used them; they used Kasen. They are not the Sazanami. Kyora auctioned their listing and died touching it. They are not Sojo’s gang. Madoka, the Daruma sorcerer, worked for the customer and died for talking. They are not the Mikaboshi. The old sorcerer kings are Part 2’s island problem. The Hishaku are a present-tense conspiracy that stole a war’s leftovers and then, at the last hour, handed the leftovers back to the man who had already spent 200,000 civilians.

After Yura’s body is no longer Yura’s, the ten do not automatically become Akemura’s squad. Possession is not a merger. This page will not invent a new org chart the magazine has not printed. What is printed: flame tattoos, fire-gate, eight names, two still unlabeled, a raid, a sale, an auction listing, a hotel kidnapping, a headquarters attack, a cell conversation, a body offered. The factions index places them next to Kamunabi and Sazanami. The technique catalog places their arts next to the blades they stole.
