Jump 2023 #42 to now

Publication record刊行

From an ironic Big Three meme to four million copies, a Next Manga Award, and a Cypic series dated April 2027.

Kagurabachi is written and illustrated by Takeru Hokazono. It began in Weekly Shōnen Jump issue 2023 #42, on 19 September 2023. Jump Comics Volume 1, Mission, landed 2 February 2024 (ISBN 978-4-08-883819-9). As of 1 May 2026 there are eleven Japanese tankōbon. Volume 12 is listed for 4 September 2026 (ISBN 978-4-08-885177-8). VIZ Media and MANGA Plus carry the English simulpub the same week as the magazine. VIZ’s first print volume arrived 5 November 2024 (ISBN 978-1-9747-4724-5). English Volume 9 is listed for 3 November 2026. Volumes 10 and 11 English are still TBD. This page is the circulation and awards record. The volume guide is the ISBN table. The chapter index is the title list through 129.

How it landed

Promo art was a black coat, white type, and a fish. English Twitter decided it was the next Big Three before page one existed. Chapter 1 was the most-viewed new title on MANGA Plus in its first week. The series passed Spy × Family, Dragon Ball Super, and Boruto on that app’s popularity ranking. By April 2024 MANGA Plus had logged over 99 million page views. Ironic posts opened the chapter. The Vs. Sojo fight, Hakuri, and Samura are why people stayed. Hokazono was born in Osaka on 6 September 2000. The one-shot Enten placed at the 100th Tezuka Awards and ran in Jump Giga Spring 2021. Then Giga and Jump shorts: Farewell! Cherry Boy!, Chain, Madogiwa de Amu, Roku no Meiyaku. The serial is the first long work. Author file: Hokazono. Meme file: meme to flagship.

Kōhei Horikoshi and Masashi Kishimoto have recommended the book. Vaundy has too. It ranked seventh on AnimeJapan’s 2024 Most Wanted Anime Adaptation poll. Da Vinci “Book of the Year”: 22nd in 2024, 13th in 2025. Sixth on Takarajimasha’s Kono Manga ga Sugoi! 2025 list for male readers. The 2023 joke is now a shelf that Circana BookScan and the New York Times graphic list have had to make room for, monthly, since late 2024.

Circulation

Shueisha’s restated numbers, because the meme predicted the opposite:

  • July 2024: over 350,000 copies (including digital)
  • August 2024: over 600,000
  • October 2024: over 1 million
  • December 2024: over 1.3 million
  • May 2025: over 2.2 million
  • October 2025: over 3 million sold
  • April 2026: over 4 million in circulation

North America: Circana BookScan monthly top 20 adult graphic novels since November 2024 (Volume 1 hit #1 that month). New York Times Graphic Books and Manga bestseller monthly list since December 2024. French edition via Kana, noted on Volume 11 promo. Other territories follow local Jump licenses. Buy the tankōbon. We do not list scans. Collectibles is the shelf page.

Awards

2024: won the 10th Next Manga Award, print category. Nominated for the 70th Shogakukan Manga Award. 2025: nominated for the 49th Kodansha Manga Award (shōnen) and for the Eisner Award, Best U.S. Edition of International Material, Asia. 2026: won the Daruma for Best Action Manga at the Japan Expo Awards; nominated for Best Drawing and New Manga. Crunchyroll took seven Daruma at Japan Expo 2026’s 25th edition; Kagurabachi’s action win is the one this page records. Nominations are not a personality. The Next Manga win is the one that matches the meme-to-flagship arc.

Anime

December 2024: Toyo Keizai reported Cypic (then CygamesPictures) via CyberAgent and Shochiku. April 2026: confirmed as a television series, Tetsuya Takeuchi directing, Keigo Sasaki on character designs, April 2027. First twenty minutes on a world tour from July 2026 (Anime Expo, Japan Expo, AnimagiC, Anime NYC), full first episode in Japan Q2 2027. Crunchyroll worldwide except Japan, mainland China, North Korea, South Korea. Muse Communication in South and Southeast Asia. Cast announced so far: Taihi Kimura (Chihiro; voiced comic: Shoya Ishige), Tomokazu Seki (Kunishige; voiced comic: Kenta Fujimaki), Katsuyuki Konishi (Shiba; voiced comic: Jun Fukushima). Hinao’s voiced-comic credit is Akari Tadano. More names will land closer to 2027. See the anime.
