Quoted名台詞

The sentences the book taught the room to say, with the stills they belong to.

Sunday quotes a panel, then argues about the translation. This page keeps the ones that survived being memed: Hokazono on fins, VIZ on True Realm, Hakuri calling himself empty, Enten as a retraction. Pictures first. The essays are linked where the line is doing work. Official chapters remain on VIZ and MANGA Plus. We paraphrase where a verbatim balloon would be a scan.

“I really intended to go with koi until the last minute.”
Takeru Hokazono, Asahi interview, on Enten. Goldfish won because the bowl looked like a life and the fins did not look chubby. Goldfish page.
“In a revenge tale where the protagonist’s clothes are stained black with blood, the colors white, black, and red match well.”
Same interview. Volume 1’s jacket is that sentence printed. Later books invite one extra hue when a rival blade is on stage.
True Realm is the brief, meant.
The wielder’s intent, locked to the steel. Sojo wanted slaughter; Cloud Gouger gained it. Chihiro wanted a household object to cut a weapon. True Realm essay.
He arrives “empty.” The Storehouse was already his.
Hakuri was scattering his own spirit energy. The auction taught him he was stock. The discarded son inherited the building. Storehouse.
He loved the work and never met the man.
Sojo as Kunishige’s closest misreader: a customer who loved the blades. The bathhouse extra is the same curiosity as the crater. The essay.
Enten cuts Cloud Gouger.
Wartime swords were sold as immortal. Chapter 18 files a correction. Chihiro keeps the pieces. Even a dead blade can still spend a few charges of Mei.
A skeleton on a permission slip.
Flame Bone up to the ribs. Full-body is a rumor the videos like. The auction ends because she will burn the hall rather than leave people in the Storehouse. Flame Bone.
Owl over Japan.
Tobimune as a support blade that turns the country into a room. Chapter 60’s elevator is a door. Owl essay.
Enten is a retraction that looks like a life.
Kunishige could not smash the wartime steel, so he made a retraction that looks like a life. Chapter 83 is where the brief is spoken. What Enten was for.
Give the war its chapters.
The English room’s brief for Part 2: talks, princess, fire, Ironworks. Twenty to forty chapters of ground. Part 2.
