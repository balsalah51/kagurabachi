Three years ago

The raid襲撃

Three Hishaku sorcerers in a house. A smith dead in front of his son. Six wartime blades gone. Enten still in the boy’s hand.

The Hishaku formed about four years before the present with one plan for Shinuchi. A year later they hit the Rokuhira workshop. The book opens after that night and spends a hundred-plus chapters teaching who ordered it, who walked in, who leaked the address, and what the son did with the sword that stayed. The raid is not a prologue fight. It is the present-tense plot’s unfinished business. Place: the workshop. Org: Hishaku.

Who walked in

Three sorcerers. Hokuto in samurai clothes, armored puppet, the man who also murdered Ibuki Misaka so Cloud Gouger’s contract would open. Uran, ice, breath that freezes and restrains. A third raider still unnamed among the ten. They did not need the fire-gate for a workshop massacre. Three people in a house is enough. Yura ordered it. He is the mind, not necessarily one of the three in the room. File: Yura.

Kunishige dies at 37, birthday June 5, in front of Chihiro. The six wartime Enchanted Blades leave the cellar he spent years trying to smash. Enten stays with the son. That is the whole inventory change. The public still thinks there are six swords. The Kamunabi want six. The seventh was never on their books.

The leak

Kasen leaked the address. Director, barriers and sealing, one of the people who sealed Shinuchi after the war with Ichiki and Yatsuru. He believed Enchanted Blades could create order. He hired the Hishaku as a path back to the steel. The Hishaku used him as a path to the house. The Kamunabi learn this later. The raid is already three years old when the leak has a name in the magazine. Essay-adjacent: the leak.

Azami and Shiba had hidden Kunishige after Malediction. Ichiki helped. The bureau that filed 200,000 civilians as victory also hid the smith who confiscated the weapons. Kasen’s faction wanted those weapons used. Those two bureaus shared a building until Yura walked to the cell. The raid is what happens when one director decides the hide is the problem.

The same campaign

Ibuki dies in the same campaign, not necessarily in the same room. Cloud Gouger’s Lifelong Contract opens. Three years later Yura sells the weather to Sojo in early October. The remaining wartime bearers go into Sanso fortresses: Kokugoku, Senkutsuji, Subaru relocated when the pattern becomes obvious. The state’s idea of safety is a box. The Hishaku’s idea of a contract is a kill. Page: Sanso.

Chihiro is fifteen when the house falls, eighteen when the book starts. He and Shiba spend three years in the Tokyo underworld. Cafe Haru Haru is the sit-down between jobs. The hunt is recover the swords and kill the men who ordered the raid. He does not start knowing his uncle is the Sword Master or his mother is a princess in a fire. The book is the education. Underworld: the street.

What the raid bought

Six blades the Hishaku cannot fully use while the bearers live. A seventh they never took. A son who treats goldfish as partners. A customer for Cloud Gouger. A listing for Shinuchi at the 208th Rakuzaichi after Yura cracks the seal. A private paper with Samura. A hotel kidnapping aimed at a daughter. A headquarters assault aimed at a cell. The raid is the first move in that sequence, not the last.

Yura later says out loud that he orchestrated Kunishige’s murder. The chapter that holds that line is the one the fandom still sends. Official pages: VIZ / MANGA Plus. Timeline: world. Premise, shorter: a cellar he could not smash.

What chapter 1 does with this night

The first issue does not stage the raid as a tournament fight. It stages the aftermath: Chihiro on the floor, Enten already in his hands, then a cut to the eighteen-year-old already working. Close reading: “Mission”. The encyclopedia’s job on this page is the inventory the first issue cannot stop to file — who walked in, who leaked, who died in the same campaign, which blade stayed.

Kunishige’s death is the first named grave on the ledger. The three years after it are the Tokyo street. The present-tense book is still that night’s unfinished business, even when the rooms change to an auction house, a hotel, and a government basement. Calendar: October to the war.
