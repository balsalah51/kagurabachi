Manga

Takeru Hokazono (外薗 健). Born 6 September 2000, Osaka. Kagurabachi is his first long work, Weekly Shōnen Jump 2023 issue 42. Earlier: Tezuka Award Enten (different kanji, different story), Roku no Meiyaku, other Giga and Jump shorts. He talked to a working swordsmith so the workshop and Part 2’s kiln would not be cosplay.
