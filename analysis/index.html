Enten, True Realm, the Malediction, Sojo, Flame Bone.

The book keeps asking the same question in different rooms. If you love the blades, who are you loyal to, the steel, or the person who was sorry he made it? Enten is the father’s answer: a retraction, not a seventh trophy. The Malediction is the uncle’s answer: a field the size of a nation, filed as victory. Revenge is the son’s first engine. Inheritance is the forge he actually gets. Irishima’s vein is Part 2’s answer: the war was always a mineral, and the kiln is now on the page. Owl is the support blade that makes Japan a room. The leak is stationery. The titles were doing the politics before the essays did. Play asks whether you respect the chair. Copying is a smith’s son shutting his eyes in a Hishaku’s hallway.

Sojo is the reader who never met the household. Hiyuki is the state as a person you might, on a good day, stand next to. The goldfish are furniture first. Every later monster is a larger version of the Vs. Sojo argument: a government squad that is not enough, a fan who will spend civilians, a protagonist who will not. The cards below are essays, not recaps. The recaps live on the arc map. The videos people send live on the theories page.
