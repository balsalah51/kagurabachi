Overview

Yojiro Sengoku runs the Kyoto Bloodshed Hotel. Reigen One-Sword Style. He taught the staff. The Masumi pick the building as a veil when Samura’s Owl is up and the Hishaku want Iori. Bloodshed is the house’s business and then the week’s weather. Sengoku dies so Toto can read a head. That sentence is the hotel’s ethics in one cut: a swordsman who taught a style becomes a page in a tracker’s book.

Itsuo Shirakai’s Iai White Purity is lids-down speed from a mountain bigot. Reigen is a house style, a hotel curriculum. Iai is Itsuo’s mountain. Enchanted Blades are Kunishige’s workshop. Chihiro copies both in the same corridors: Kuguri’s contempt, the staff’s curriculum, Iori’s body already knowing a different school under a seal. Sengoku is the landlord of that classroom. He does not survive the lesson.

The school

A hotel that trains its people is a fortress the Masumi can rent as darkness. Easy Does It, the operation name, is the book being funny about a week that includes a beheading. Sumi’s motorcycle is the road in. Sengoku’s Reigen is the building. Chihiro’s decision to tell Iori the truth rather than re-seal it is why the veil is also a confession. Kuguri stays because the fake Iai is good enough to take seriously. Toto holds the clock. Hiruhiko does not respect objects. Play takes the upper floors apart.

Volume 8’s jacket is the silhouette under Owl: Chihiro, Iori, Hiruhiko. The house is on it. Sengoku stays inside the building. Recaps that mention “the Kyoto hotel” without the man who taught it are doing the graves the same courtesy they do the Steam Squad: a building, no teacher.

The sample

Toto reads blood. A severed head is a sample with a style still on it. She is there for Iori. Sengoku is inventory. The kidnapping still fails, because Iori shields Ikura and the seal breaks. Toto did not fail the read. The job failed the girl. Samura arrives because two Enchanted Blades in one hotel is a beacon Owl can see. Feathers, banquet, goldfish. The father occupies the same city as the daughter again. The hotelier is already a page.

Ro of the Masumi later recovers Kumeyuri. Logistics continues. Sengoku does not. See the hotel page. Official chapters: VIZ / MANGA Plus.
