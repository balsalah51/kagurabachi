Yura and Hiruhiko幽と昼彦

Not the same job. One lists. One wrecks hotels.

The mind

The Hishaku are eight printed names and two still unlabeled. Yura ordered the raid, sold a sword, listed a masterpiece, signed Samura, walked to the cell, and offered a body. He is not the loudest killer in the set of ten. He is the one who decides.

Hiruhiko is eighteen, Blood Crane, Play, the hotel wrecker. He hits the Sanso. He drops a building. He is what the flame tattoo looks like when the mind wants noise. Toto tracks by blood. Hokuto wants a real fight. Kuguri courts a blade. Yukisada sits in a barrier. Bingo eats and sleeps. Uran froze the raid.

The offer

Yura spends Shinuchi without drawing it and offers Akemura a body. Hiruhiko’s Play is a different spend: architecture as contempt. The mind and the loud one share a clan and a fire-gate. They do not share a brief. See Yura and Hishaku.
