Bandai’s UNION ARENA put Kagurabachi into cardboard while the manga is still in Jump. English set code UE16BT (KGR-1). Japanese packs landed December 2025; the English booster followed 27 February 2026. Official product page: unionarena-tcg.com. Every card uses manga art, plus alternate illustrations Hokazono drew for the game. A serialized Chihiro chase sits on top of the Super Rare ladder.

This page is the encyclopedia file for that one set. Pictures of the singles English tables actually look for: Chihiro’s Super Rare, the Chihiro engine, Togo Shiba, Hakuri, Char, Kyora, Hiyuki, Iori, Yura, and Iai White Purity Style. The booster

Twelve cards a pack. Four rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Super Rare, plus Action Point cards at a low rate. Bandai’s count is 82 types and 13 AP types. Alt-arts are in the mix. A display box is 16 packs; a case is 16 boxes. English MSRP on the pack was $4.99. Box toppers are five foil designs: two Chihiro, Hakuri, Iori, Sojo. Tournament-legal from the regional pre-release date.

English tables have been playing Red Chihiro as the competitive color. Purple is the Kyora side of the set. This file does not keep a decklist; the meta moves. The pictures below are the faces people still search.

Who is on the cardboard

The set walks Part 1: Vs. Sojo, the Rakuzaichi, Sword Bearer Assassination, the Kyoto hotel. Chihiro and Enten are the Red face. Hakuri is the Storehouse made into a card. Shiba is the teleport the competitive pile still wants. Char is the regeneration the engine recurs. Kyora is the Purple auction. Hiyuki is the Kamunabi’s pointed end. Iori is the daughter whose seal the hotel broke. Yura is the Hishaku mind. Iai White Purity Style is the school Samura and Uruha learned with their eyes closed; Chihiro copies it. Character files: Chihiro, Hakuri, Shiba, Char, Kyora, Hiyuki, Iori, Yura. The school: Iai White Purity.

Hokazono drew new illustrations for the product. A star-parallel Super Rare of Chihiro is the expensive chase; a serial-numbered version of the ★★★ design exists in a hundred copies. Those are collector facts, not a price guide. Prices move. Look at the listing.
