What the page spends

Three Hishaku sorcerers hit the Rokuhira workshop. Kunishige is killed in front of Chihiro. The six wartime Enchanted Blades leave the cellar. Enten, the seventh, stays with the son. Off the same plan, Hokuto murders Ibuki Misaka so Cloud Gouger’s Lifelong Contract opens. Uran is ice on the breath that night. Yura is the mind who ordered it.

The workshop was isolation after the war: house, forge, cellar Kunishige could not smash, a bowl of goldfish. Enten was forged here about fifteen years after the Seitei War as a retraction, not a state trophy. The raid turns a household into a hunt. Chihiro and Shiba go into the Tokyo underworld for three years.
