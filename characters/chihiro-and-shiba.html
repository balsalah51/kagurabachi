Chihiro and Shibaチヒロと登吾

Shiba is the adult who would not let the smith quit. Then he would not let the son hunt alone.

Before the raid

Togo Shiba is a Soga guardian in the war book and a Kamunabi sorcerer after it. He believes Kunishige’s eyes are the only way to make Datenseki into a blade instead of a crater. He is right. He leaves the bureau when the smith hides. Azami stays. That split is the first politics the household teaches.

Teleportation is the art. Cafe Haru Haru is the civilian door. Hinao keeps the lights on and brokers sorcerers so the coat does not have to be loud every hour. For three years after the raid, Chihiro and Shiba work the Tokyo underworld. The hunt is a job with meals. Chapter 5 is A Good Meal. The plate is method.

What he will not spend

Shiba dumps the headquarters fight onto the street when the building can no longer hold it. He is already the friend who will not let Kunishige quit in the kiln chapters. Part 2 puts him back on Irishima as a guardian while the son is still the present-tense coat. The pair is not a gag about an uncle. It is the book’s argument that the household needed a third adult who could leave a room.

Birthday, printed: 15 October. Voice: Katsuyuki Konishi. See Shiba, Cafe Haru Haru, and teleport.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
