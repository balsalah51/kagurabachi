Chihiro and Hiyukiチヒロと緋雪

She is how the bureau wishes it looked. He is why the wish is not enough.

The auction house introduction

Hiyuki Kagari enters as the Kamunabi’s pointed end. Flame Bone of the Starving is inherited kit on a permission slip. Chapter 20 titles her The Kamunabi’s Weapon. Chapter 21 is Lukewarm. The auction is not hot yet. Tafuku Mihara is the partner the first meetings leave as a caption: duel domain, two people, one match, then the street returns.

Chihiro is already inside the building the state wanted sealed from him. He lets the house take Enten so the blade can scout the Kura on its own charge. He walks back in with Cloud Gouger’s stump. Hiyuki’s skeleton meets Kyora’s warehouse. The lesson she and Tafuku both learn is the same one the Anti-Cloud Gouger Special Forces died teaching: this boy will spend himself before he spends the civilians in the room.

After the firm ends

The deal is Magatsumi to the state, Enten in the son’s hand, Chihiro on the books. That assumes a director who wants blades sealed. Kasen wanted them used. A skeleton on a permission slip does not beat Magatsumi. The perfect act is not the HQ basement’s tool. The basement does not use referees.

Hiyuki remains the state’s best argument that it is not only Kasen. Chihiro remains the household’s argument that the state is late. They stand next to each other more often than the first book promised. They do not become the same job. See Hiyuki, Tafuku, and Flame Bone.
