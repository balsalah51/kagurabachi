What is Kagurabachi?

Kagurabachi (カグラバチ) is Takeru Hokazono’s Weekly Shōnen Jump manga: a revenge story about Chihiro Rokuhira, Enchanted Blades forged from Datenseki, and the war those swords actually ended. It began in Jump 2023 issue 42. This site is the independent encyclopedia for that one series. The series page and premise are the short version; the story so far is the commute.

Who is Chihiro Rokuhira?

The swordsmith’s son. He inherited Enten, the seventh Enchanted Blade, after the Hishaku killed Kunishige and stole the six wartime swords. Later he learns why the seventh blade was forged at all. File: Chihiro Rokuhira. The steel: Enten. The brief: what Enten was for.

What are Enchanted Blades in Kagurabachi?

Enchanted Blades (妖刀, yōtō) are katana Kunishige Rokuhira cut from Datenseki. Spirit energy leaves the body as a shape: goldfish, clouds, flowers, feathers. Most have three named techniques and a True Realm. Seven exist. Magatsumi is Shinuchi, the masterpiece. Start at the blade encyclopedia, the beginner blades page, and the technique catalog.

What does KB stand for?

KB is the common short for Kagurabachi. The official anime accounts use it too: @kb_anime_jp, @kb_anime_en. On this site, KB, カグラバチ, and Kagurabachi all search the same workshop. Try search for KB.

What is Kagurabachi Part 2?

Part 2 is the Seitei War book, from chapter 116. Irishima, Chiaki Soga, the talks, Datenseki, the kiln. Part 1 closed the present-tense hunt; Part 2 is the war that made the cellar. Pages: Part 2, Seitei War arc, Irishima.
