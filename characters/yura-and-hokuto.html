Yura and Hokuto由良と北斗

A director and a fighter. One jacket, four men.

The four-man photograph

Volume 10, The Swordsmen, puts Natsuki, Hokuto, Uruha, and Yura on one jacket. Cover studies already call it a war photograph. Hokuto’s printed jobs sit next to Natsuki. Yura’s sit next to Kasen, Hiruhiko, and Akemura. This room is the Hishaku half of that photograph.

Hokuto is not a blank seat. Yura is not only a title. Flame tattoos still mark the set. See Yura, Hokuto, Hokuto and Natsuki.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
