Name register人名録

A directory, not a wiki dump. Names without a solo file live here until Jump gives them a room.

The character encyclopedia has solo files for Chihiro’s circle, the famous bearers, and the 2026 desks (Hokuto, Kuguri, Natsuki, Kasen, Subaru, Tafuku, Kiri, Ibuki). This register is everyone else the scrape could source, plus a one-line reminder of the people who already have pages. Two Hishaku and two wartime blades stay unlabeled. This archive will not invent them. Official chapters: VIZ / MANGA Plus.

Rokuhira house

Chihiro Rokuhira (六平 千鉱). 18. 11 August. Enten. Taihi Kimura / Shoya Ishige. Kunishige Rokuhira (六平 国重). Seven blades. Tomokazu Seki / Kenta Fujimaki. Chiaki Soga (曽我 千晃). Mother. Princess Soga. Foresight. Togo Shiba (柴 登吾). Teleport. Katsuyuki Konishi / Jun Fukushima. Hinao (ヒナオ). Cafe Haru Haru. Akari Tadano in the voiced comic. Connects sorcerers to yakuza and corporations who need them. The civilian door. Voices: desk.

Enchanted Blade bearers

Akemura Soga, Magatsumi, Sword Master. Seiichi Samura, Tobimune. Yoji Uruha, Kumeyuri. Ibuki Misaka, Cloud Gouger, dead. Subaru Urita (瓜田 すば琉), unnamed blade, Sushi Subaru, Sand-Bone One-Sword Style, duplication, hachimaki, mustache, scar over the right eye, prolific smith before the war, helped Kunishige start the project, relocated after Sanso attacks. One war-ensemble portrait still unlabeled. Two wartime swords still unnamed. Chihiro holds Enten and held dying Cloud Gouger. Iori holds Tobimune after Samura. Hiruhiko held Kumeyuri until Ro recovered it. Kyora and Yura were Magatsumi proxies, not bearers.

Kamunabi leadership

Kasen (嘉仙), director, leak, barriers. Ichiki (壱鬼), elderly, small, trained Shiba and Azami, helped hide Kunishige, sealed Shinuchi. Yatsuru (夜弦), sole woman among named leaders, barriers and sealing, Shinuchi seal. Soshiro Azami (薊 奏士郎), Coin (Koin), strongest head, clinic art rewritten as execution. Izaru (亥猿), mutton chops, rosary bead chains, distrusts Chihiro, talks as if Kunishige stole the blades. Kudo (区堂), gas-mask apparatus, one eye closed, Warrior’s Path (Shitō), sends a body through walls, dies protecting Hakuri. White Robes: Kasen, Ichiki, Yatsuru. Full org: Kamunabi.

Kamunabi operations

Hiyuki Kagari, Flame Bone of the Starving. Tafuku Mihara (美原 多福), duel domain, sumo silhouette, calm. Natsuki Misaka (巳坂奈ツ基), Lightning Menace (Raiku), squadron leader, Ibuki’s brother, Kumeyuri candidate, Volume 10 jacket. Kiri Shirakai (白廻 斬), squadron leader, two-meter odachi, five-shaku blade, chapter 90, granddaughter of Itsuo, vow to decapitate him. Fushimi (伏見), Kokugoku Steam Squad, Smoke Axe (煙斧), cuts with smoke between hands, beats Datenseki troops, dies to Hiruhiko with the rest of the squad.

Anti-Cloud Gouger Special Forces, six people, one sword, four graves. Desk: ACG. Ikuto Hagiwara (萩原 幾兎), commander, Jikai (磁戒), magnetism, loses both legs, later hallucinates Kugara, chapter 98’s cruel title, still useful in HQ. Hajime Kugara (具柄 一), iron body, mask, childhood friend of Hagiwara since age five, dies, the hallucination’s face. Kazane Machi (真智 カザネ), newest, Demon Monster (怪魑, Kaichi), the unused secret weapon, Sojo takes the right arm first. Shiyumi Harima (張間 梓弓), Gansui (岩垂), stone. Kiyohiko Uzuki (卯月 清彦), binding spells. Makoto Kasahara (笠原 誠), enlarged hands. Two survivors. That was the cavalry.

Masumi (巻墨): Ro (郎), leader, over 23, child appearance, sunglasses and candy cigarettes copied off Samura, “get fired up,” recovers Kumeyuri, looks after Chihiro at Hakuri’s request. Moku (杢), tall, train station, feeds Chihiro when the boy starts eating himself, rallies Senkutsuji. Sumi (炭), cross tattoo on the right face, transportation mandalas drawn with a marker, motorcycle away from Kuguri, tends Hakuri so Storehouse can be used safely. They serve Samura, then are freed, then protect Iori in Operation: Easy Does It. Core Station Surveyor and operators, West Entrance Commander, Ishihira, Kashima: named as HQ furniture in tropes lists, not given rooms here.

Hishaku

Ten. Flame tattoos. Fire-gate. Eight printed. Yura (幽), leader. Hokuto (北兜), puppet armor, Ibuki’s killer. Hiruhiko (昼彦), Blood Crane, Play. Kuguri (久々李), Twilight Wave, unrequited blade. Toto (斗斗), blood tracking, fire-gate as habit, Iori job, Sengoku’s head as a sample. Uran (右嵐), ice, breath that freezes, raid on Kunishige. Bingo (瓶伍), Mako (魔咬), lion-dancer lucky charms, stacked weight on destroyers, eats corpses, gets sleepy. Yukisada (幸禎), 17, strongest fighter per Yura, regeneration past decapitation, Vessel in the HQ barrier. Two unnamed. Korogumi yakuza as allied muscle. Org: Hishaku.

Sazanami

Kyora, eleventh head. Hakuri, dual inheritance. Soya (漣 宗也), heir, obsessive, amnesia after the fall, volume extra about memories he wants gone. Tenri (漣 天理), short blades, Datenseki pop, jellyfish. Tamaki (漣 珠紀), older sister, lies to Kyora about Soya. Enji (漣 円慈), begs Shiba for death, is told to live. Org: Sazanami. Storehouse: Kura. Yuu Inazuma / Mr. Inazuma (稲妻 裕), child trying to break into the auction for his sister; Chihiro gets her out.

Soga and Mikaboshi

Chiaki, Akemura, already filed. Hiroto Soga (曽我 昼音), clan head, Kurotsuchi, directional gravity, levitate or crush, among the strongest before the war, killed on Irishima by Ariu. Yoshinojo Soga (曽我 義之丞), older, mustache and beard, cocky grin, unrelenting, killed by Ariu on the same island. Giyu Soga (曽我 義勇), Hiroto’s ambitious younger brother, next in line, willing to accept Mikaboshi demands that include handing Chiaki over. Ukizane Soga, historical, nearly destroyed the Mikaboshi before they hid under the sea. Ariu Mikaboshi (亜利雨 箕加星), crown prince, Sumika (栖), insect constructs, poisoned air, Datenseki-hardened body. The Mikaboshi king who built the undersea branch habitat is not given a modern name on this desk. Camp: Magatsumi copies Ariu. Not a caption.

Sorcery Bureau, still in Part 2

Shuji Mashiro (真城 秀治), Shiba’s partner, Akuu (空亜), air pressure, hands-free weapons, gifted a Kunishige sword at 18, opposes taking stolen Datenseki to the smith, dies later to Ariu, alive in the talks. Shinsaku Hasumi (蓮水 晋作), lab chief, comes to trust Kunishige, later resigns after letting Shiba steal ore, disillusioned when the bureau accepts Mikaboshi ceasefire terms. Joji (丈治), eyemask, nose ring, ranked above Shiba and Mashiro, annoyed. The Bureau becomes an army, then the Kamunabi. Part 2 is before that rename has finished meaning anything.

Other named

Char Kyonagi (鏡凪 シャル), last of the regenerative clan. Her mother, hunted with her, not given a separate file. Norisaku Madoka (円 法炸), Daruma sorcerer, Sojo’s employee, exploding Daruma, decides to go straight, killed by Sojo for talking. Chapter 8’s title is his name and a promise. Itsuo Shirakai (白廻逸夫), Iai founder, mountains, texts, bigot. Yojiro Sengoku (戦国与次郎), Kyoto Bloodshed Hotel, Reigen One-Sword Style, taught the staff, dies so Toto can read a head. Hotel desk: Kyoto. Ikura (井倉), Iori’s classmate, loner, trails Toto, helps the seal break. The Shigyu brothers (死柳兄弟), hired chaos, eleven Kamunabi dead, Azami kills both. Kudo dies for Hakuri. Goggles sorcerer on Natsuki’s Togo Squad: tropes name, no room. Pine-sorcery Hishaku hirelings at Senkutsuji: method, not names.

Sojo (双城 厳一) already has the loudest file on the customer side. This register exists so the index stops pretending the war, the bureau, and the ten were four famous faces. Who is who is the short map. This is the long one. Techniques attached to these names: catalog.
