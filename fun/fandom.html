A hashtag that had to become a reading group. Then an anime tag.

The meme page is the 2023 origin: ironic Big Three posts, then 99 million Manga Plus views. This page is what the room does now. Sunday still lives under one tag. The legal way to be there is VIZ or MANGA Plus the day Jump drops. The Sunday ritual is the how.

What people argue

Craft versus prediction. Goldfish-as-language (correct about Kuro / Aka / Nishiki, wrong about carp). True Realm as the wielder, not a gauge. Sojo as Kunishige’s worst reader. Enten as a retraction. Those hold up against the printed pages and have essays here.

Camps: Cour 1 ends at the elevator (chapter 60’s doors, still a camp). Reforge Enten versus a new named blade. The Seitei War flashback has to be long enough. Ariu as Magatsumi’s original. Those live on the theories page as arguments the room is having.

What is worth keeping

ANN on Anime NYC, Hokazono in a stuffed room that was not billed as a main event. Asahi on the fins and the Hollywood-Japan crush. Weekend Weebs on chapter 83. ComicBook picking up the goldfish translation from @brkagurabachi. The useful split is: interview and chapter, or leak thumbnail. Only the first kind gets a card.

Official tags now include #BachiAnime. @kb_anime_jp and @kb_anime_en are the studio’s voices. The 2023 posts did not plan an April 2027 Cypic show. The pages did. See the anime page.

How this site sits in it

Wiki-depth articles, a Kanzenshuu-style manga guide, cover studies, long essays. Chapters live on VIZ and MANGA Plus. If you are sending the book to someone who only knows the hashtag, send first-read notes and chapter 1 on MANGA Plus.

Circulation passed 4 million by April 2026. Next Manga Award 2024 (print). The fandom’s job now is the same as Chihiro’s: stop treating the work as a trophy. Read it. The pictures that job produced, memes, teaser stills, Kuro in the rain, live on the Sunday board and the wall.
