Already on the page

Kagurabachi serializes in Weekly Shōnen Jump. Chapter 1, “Mission” (すべきこと), ran in issue 2023 #42 on 19 September. VIZ and MANGA Plus carry the English simulpub the same week. English titles in this table follow VIZ and common wiki renderings. Japanese titles follow Jump. When those two disagree, “Daybreak” landing on both 夜更け and 黎明, two different “Deadlock”s, two different “Future”s, the Japanese column is the magazine’s word and the English column is the one readers search.

The title language is the book’s method in miniature. Early chapters are objects and meals: “Heaps,” “A Good Meal,” “Peace,” “Food,” “Tea.” Then the swords get their names on the page: “Enten vs. Cloud Gouger,” “True Realm,” “Roar.” The auction speaks in architecture and family: “Storehouse,” “Deal,” “Equal,” “Fervent,” “The Curtain Falls.” The long book names people, “Uruha,” “Samura,” “Iori,” “Natsuki”, and then puts quotation marks on the words the press already used: “Strongest,” “Sword Master,” “Heroes.” Part 2 opens on a rank and a place. “Princess.” “The Irishima Talks,” four times, then “END.” “Chiaki.” “Smelting.” “Fire.” “Ironworks.” “I'm Fine!” “Tipping Point.” “Misaka.”
