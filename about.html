Aboutこのサイトについて

A fan site for Takeru Hokazono’s Kagurabachi. Nothing else.

A fan site for Takeru Hokazono’s Kagurabachi. Nothing else. Characters, blades, volumes, chapters, and a few essays. New chapters live on VIZ and MANGA Plus — we do not host them.

What is here

  • Guide — the homepage rows written out: series, premise, blades, story, on paper.
  • Characters — Chihiro’s circle, the blade bearers, Kamunabi, Hishaku, Sazanami. Also Chiaki Soga and Azami.
  • Enchanted Blades — Enten, Cloud Gouger, Magatsumi, Kumeyuri, Tobimune. Two wartime swords stay unnamed.
  • Manga guide — volume ISBNs, chapter index through 126, jacket studies, color pages.
  • Story arcs — Vs. Sojo, Rakuzaichi, Sword Bearer Assassination, Seitei War.
  • World — timeline, glossary, locations, contracts, Iai, Datenseki, sorcery. Factions.
  • Essays — Enten, Malediction, revenge, Sojo, Flame Bone, True Realm.
  • Fun — goldfish, meme, English fandom, names, Hokazono. Anime. Theories. Collectibles.

Official reading

Read the serial on VIZ / Shonen Jump or MANGA Plus (title 100274). Buy tankōbon from Shueisha (Jump Comics) and VIZ. Anime information sits at anime.kagurabachi.jp — Cypic, Tetsuya Takeuchi, April 2027, Crunchyroll outside the listed exceptions.

This is not an official Shueisha or VIZ page. It is a fan encyclopedia. Art on these pages is Hokazono / Shueisha (anime: Project Kagurabachi). Late story beats sit behind black spoiler bars you can click.
