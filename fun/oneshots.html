Bathhouse & other extrasお風呂と余録

Jump’s meanest arms dealer is also a man who will tour tubs. That is not a contradiction in this book.

Genichi Sojo’s Bathhouse Quest

Two parts, packed with the tankōbon: Sōjō Gen’ichi no Ofuro Tanbō. Sojo, off the murder clock, rates baths. It is the same curiosity that makes him a Kunishige scholar. He wants to know how a thing is built — a blade, a body, a tub. English readers who only met him as “the Cloud Gouger guy” meet a sitcom villain who still sounds like himself. That is why the extra works. It does not apologize for chapter 18. It reminds you he was a person with hobbies while he was a disaster.

Soya Sazanami’s Memories, Begone!

Sazanami Sōya no Modoranai de Kioku. The older brother who treated Hakuri as defective stock gets a gag about memories he would like to misplace. It is not a redemption. It is the clan’s emotional illiteracy as a four-page joke. Read it after the Rakuzaichi if you want the auction house to feel like a family instead of a dungeon map.

Buy the volumes for these. We do not host them. They are the Kanzenshuu-style reason the tankōbon is not just the magazine with a spine. See the volume guide.
