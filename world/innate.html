Yōjutsu

Innate sorcery固有の妖術

The arts that stay in the body. A Lifelong Contract shuts them off. That is why some people never sign.

Enchanted Blades overfill. Spirit energy leaves as goldfish, clouds, flowers, feathers. Innate sorcery stays. The same kanji, 妖, sits on both words. The difference is whether the art came back. When a bearer signs a Lifelong Contract, the old art goes dark until someone cuts the knot. Uruha’s Crimson Recital is the clean example: the banquet sword is the job; the recital is the person, and it waits. Named blade techniques live on the catalog. This page is everyone who fights without becoming weather.

Extraction and the street

Teleportation, Togo Shiba. Famous as a teenager. Extraction, infiltration, civilian evacuation, the joke until it is the only exit. He left the Kamunabi when Kunishige hid. He will not sign a blade because the contract would take this away. Cafe Haru Haru is the civilian door. Age 39. Birthday October 15.

Coin (Koin), Soshiro Azami. Family clinic stimulant rewritten as an executioner’s projectile. His father hated the rewrite. Strongest of the Kamunabi heads. Helped hide the smith. Kills the Shigyu brothers when they punch into Level 1.

Warrior’s Path (Shitō), Kudo. Send a body in a direction, through walls and floors. He spends it on Hakuri during the HQ assault and dies. The bureau’s best argument that not everyone in the building was Kasen.

Bead chains, Izaru. Restrict a target. Leadership table’s prosecutor kit. Talks as if Kunishige stole national property.

Clan houses

Isou and the Storehouse, Sazanami burial-force and subspace. Hakuri could not use Isou until he stopped scattering his own spirit energy. Then both answered. Historically almost unique in the clan: two people ever held both. He registers a person and their charged objects and moves Enchanted Blades across the country. Kyora used the Kura as a warehouse and a weapon. Page: Storehouse.

Kyonagi regeneration, Char, last of the clan. Close her own wounds and other people’s. Sojo held her as a Datenseki stabilizer. Chihiro’s group holds her as a child. Birthday December 21.

Foresight, Chiaki Soga, Princess Soga, inherited proof of Izanami. Part 2’s living warrant. The Soga pushed the Mikaboshi off the mainland with this kind of claim.

Kurotsuchi, Hiroto Soga, directional gravity. Killed on Irishima by Ariu Mikaboshi.

Sumika, Ariu Mikaboshi, insect constructs, poisoned air, Datenseki-hardened body. A camp treats Magatsumi as this kit recast in steel. The chapters have not confirmed that.

Kamunabi specialists

Flame Bone of the Starving, Hiyuki Kagari. Inherited skeleton, licensed by the bureau, permission slip you can see through the ribs. Stood next to Enchanted Blades in the text. Essay: Flame Bone.

Duel domain, Tafuku Mihara. Two people, one match, then the street returns. Hiyuki’s referee.

Lightning Menace (Raiku), Natsuki Misaka. Voltage in the body, not Mei. Ibuki’s brother. Squadron leader. Volume 10 jacket. A candidate for Kumeyuri who was not chosen.

Jikai, Ikuto Hagiwara, magnetism. Loses both legs against Sojo. Later hallucinates Kugara. Chapter title in the long book: “Ikuto Hagiwara, Worthless Commander.”

Smoke Axe, Fushimi, Kokugoku Steam Squad. They beat Datenseki-powered hired troops, then die to Hiruhiko.

Akuu, Mashiro, air pressure, hands-free weapons. Alive in the Irishima talks. Dies later to Ariu.

Anti-Cloud Gouger kit that is not a blade: Kazane’s Demon Monster (Kaichi), Harima’s Gansui stone, Uzuki’s binding, Kasahara’s enlarged hands, Kugara’s iron body. Four of those six die in the first book. Page: Anti-Cloud Gouger.

Hishaku kit

Armored puppet, Hokuto. Self-resembling armor, piece control. Present at Kunishige’s murder. Assassin of Ibuki.

Blood Crane (Chizuru), Hiruhiko. Razor origami, lifting Datenseki fragments. Goes quiet when he signs Kumeyuri. Killed an assailant at age three.

Twilight Wave (Hagure), Kuguri. Banks kinetic energy and heat, spends it later. Carries an Enchanted Blade without a contract.

Blood tracking, Toto. Locate and read through blood. Iori from a sample off Samura. Sengoku’s severed head the same way. Prefers the fire-gate to the cut.

Ice, Uran. Breath that freezes and restrains. One of the three in the workshop.

Mako (Demon Bite), Bingo. Lion-dancer heads. Destroying them stacks an invisible weight on whoever did the destroying. He can turn his own head into a charm and eat corpses, then gets sleepy.

Regeneration, Yukisada, seventeen. Survives decapitation. Yura calls him the strongest fighter among the ten. Became a Vessel so he could split the HQ barrier from inside.

Schools and leftovers

Iai White Purity Style is a school, not an innate art. Eyes closed. Itsuo Shirakai. Samura and Uruha are the famous students. Chihiro copies it off Kuguri and the house style at the hotel. Page: Iai.

Crimson Recital (Koen) is Uruha’s innate art, physical boost, dark while Kumeyuri is signed, back when Samura cuts the contract and keeps the man.

Subaru’s duplication: several Subarus. A surviving wartime bearer’s nightmare for anyone trying to open the contract by killing one body.

Datenseki that is not a blade still overfills and pops the user. Tenri’s jellyfish tool. Sojo’s industrial fake. Kunishige’s eyes were the only known way to make the mineral into weather instead of a crater. Page: Datenseki. Setting: sorcery.

Why this page sits beside the catalog

The technique catalog has to hold both weathers: goldfish, clouds, flowers, feathers, and the arts that never left the body. This page exists so a reader looking for “what can Shiba do” does not have to scroll past Mei and Suzaku. A Lifelong Contract is the hinge between the two lists. Sign, and the old art goes dark. Cut the knot, and it limps back. Barriers and rooms that behave like weapons live on barriers.

Yura’s talent is not a flashy innate name on this list. It is staffing. He decides which blade goes to which monster, which director gets bought, which seventeen-year-old becomes a wall. The ten’s shared fire-gate is logistics dressed as care. If you came here looking for the Hishaku’s “power,” start with the individual kits above, then read the org file.
