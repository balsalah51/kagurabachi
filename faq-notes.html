Already on the page

Kagurabachi (カグラバチ) is Takeru Hokazono’s Weekly Shōnen Jump manga: a revenge story about Chihiro Rokuhira, Enchanted Blades forged from Datenseki, and the war those swords actually ended. It began in Jump 2023 issue 42. This site is the independent encyclopedia for that one series. The series page and premise are the short version; the story so far is the commute.

It is a Kagurabachi encyclopedia with wiki-depth pages: every named character, every Enchanted Blade, volume synopses, arcs, and essays. It is not a Fandom wiki and not official. Use search the way you would a wiki search box, or walk the site map. Character files start at Chihiro’s index.
