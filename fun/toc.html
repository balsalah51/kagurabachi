The English timeline still treats a table of contents like a scoreboard. The jackets are the actual record.

A Jump week has two numbers. One is the table of contents: where the chapter sits in the magazine, a rumor about editorial love. The other is the chapter. The English room shouts “we’re so back” when the title is high and “it’s over” when the title sits lower. Hokazono is one person with a war flashback and a swordsmith’s homework. The ToC is a magazine layout. The goldfish live in the chapter.

The useful Sunday habit is still the one on the ritual page: official chapter first, then the still, then the essay. The live reaction can wait until you have seen the fish yourself. Ranking posts are a sport next to the book.

What the numbers measure

ToC position is a magazine layout. Lead color, a center spread, a last-page gag: those are real objects. “Number one forever” is a 2023 joke that some weeks comes true. Circulation is the number that survived the meme: 1 million, 2.2, 3, then 4 million by April 2026. Manga Plus views in the first months were a different sport: people actually tapping a title.

Breaks are a person drawing a kiln. After chapter 126 the book took an announced month. The hashtag will get the next chapter first. The legal page will get there the same day. Volume extras, Sojo’s tubs, Soya’s memories, live in the tankōbon, which keeps its own extras off the weekly scoreboard.

The glaze, measured

Ironic Big Three posts got chapter 1 opened. The Vs. Sojo fight, Hakuri, and Samura are why people stayed. If you are sending the book to someone who only knows a ranking screenshot, send first-read notes and chapter 1 on MANGA Plus. The Sunday wall typesets the jokes.
