Same classmate. Two jobs. One cafe later.

Who left, who stayed

Togo Shiba left the bureau when the smith hid. Soshiro Azami stayed. One of them becomes the cafe uncle with a teleport and a coat. The other becomes the man inside the building who can still move a table. Both were at the wartime edge. Both loved the same household.

The present-tense book needs both exits. Chihiro needs a guardian who is not the state. The state needs a man who did not walk out. See Azami, Shiba, Shiba and Kunishige, Azami and Kunishige.
