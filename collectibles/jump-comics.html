What the object holds

Japanese Jump Comics volumes started 2 February 2024. Eleven are out as of May 2026. Volume 12 is solicited 4 September 2026. The red-to-black spine bar, the jackets, and the extras are this object. VIZ trades are a parallel English object, not a color-matched set.

Extras: Genichi Sojo’s Bathhouse Quest (two parts), Soya Sazanami’s Memories, Begone!. Author comments have their own room. Cover studies sit on covers. Reading commutes: Volume 1 through 12. Shop stays off the homepage.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
