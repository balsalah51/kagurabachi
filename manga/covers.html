How to read a Hokazono jacket

Volume 1 sets the law: Chihiro centered, Kuro overhead, asphalt-red field, white logotype, black volume numeral. Later jackets keep that triad and invite one extra hue when a rival blade is on stage (cyan for Cloud Gouger, snow for Volume 11). Group jackets (5, 6, 10) are cast photographs in ink. Single-figure jackets (1, 9, 11) are icons.
