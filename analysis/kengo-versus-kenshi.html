A weekly rank. A jacket ensemble. Do not file them as one title.

The collision

Volume 10’s Japanese title is 剣士たち, The Swordsmen: Natsuki, Hokuto, Uruha, Yura on one photograph. Chapter 134’s weekly word is 剣豪, Masters of the Sword. English readers will hear the same hallway. The kanji are not the same job.

A third neighbor is the quoted press name 剣聖, Sword Master, already on Sword Master as press. Three English “sword” titles. Three Japanese nouns. Volume 10 room: volume 10.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
