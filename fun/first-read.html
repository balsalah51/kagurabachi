Spoiler-light through Vs. Sojo

First-read notes初読メモ

Twelve things the early chapters will not circle for you. Mild Vs. Sojo spoilers. Later arcs stay behind the black bars.

  1. The fish are furniture first. The bowl is on the table before it is a moveset. That is the point. Goldfish page.
  2. Sojo is not Hishaku. He is a customer. The men who killed Kunishige sold him a sword. A lot of recaps blur this and then cannot explain Yura.
  3. Chihiro will not spend civilians. That single rule is the whole difference between him and every adult who touched a blade in the war.
  4. Shiba is the strongest person in the room without a national weapon. The teleport is a joke until it is the only exit.
  5. Char is the test, not the mascot. If Chihiro leaves her, the book is a different genre.
  6. Datenseki explodes people. Enchanted Blades are the rare version that overflow as weather instead of craters. Sojo is trying to fake Kunishige’s eyes.
  7. True Realm is not a Super Saiyan form. It is what the wielder wants the steel to do when they finally mean it. Theory desk.
  8. Enten cuts Cloud Gouger. Wartime swords were sold as immortal. Chapter 18 files a correction.
  9. The government is not the cavalry. The Anti-Cloud Gouger squad is six specialists. Four die. That is the state’s best offer.
  10. Hakuri arrives “empty.” He is not. He was scattering his own spirit energy. The auction taught him he was stock.
  11. Hiyuki is the Kamunabi as a person you might stand next to. Flame Bone is why the auction does not become a second island.
  12. Part 1 is a revenge story that keeps naming the father. If you want the later brief — Enten as a retraction — it is seeded every time Chihiro treats the fish as partners. Samura’s “betrayal” is a contract-breaking mercy. The elevator is not the last word.

Read on VIZ or MANGA Plus. Then the arc map.
