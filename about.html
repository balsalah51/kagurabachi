Aboutこのサイトについて

A fan site for Takeru Hokazono’s Kagurabachi. Nothing else.

Characters, blades, volumes, chapters, and a few essays. New chapters live on VIZ and MANGA Plus — we do not host them.

Art on these pages is Hokazono / Shueisha (anime: Project Kagurabachi). Late story beats sit behind black spoiler bars you can click.
