One brother stayed. One classmate got the flower blade.

The seat that went the other way

Wikipedia’s character file is blunt: Natsuki was a candidate to wield Kumeyuri. Yoji Uruha was chosen instead. That, in part, is why Natsuki resents Uruha and wants equal recognition beside his brother and the other bearers. After the war Ibuki retired. Natsuki kept training.

The present-tense book puts them in the same war again. Hokuto and Natsuki share a printed pair room. Uruha and Samura share another. This room is the Kumeyuri seat that did not go to Kitakyushu. See Natsuki, Uruha, Kumeyuri.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
