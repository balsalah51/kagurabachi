The dates Jump has actually printed. Everyone else stays blank until a profile page says otherwise.

Five birthdays sit on this site because five profiles carry them. Ages are the present-tense book: Chihiro 18, Shiba 39, Sojo 30 at death, Kunishige 37 at death. Char’s age is not on her file. Other named figures — Hakuri, Hiyuki, Samura, Iori, Yura, Chiaki — do not have published dates here. This page will grow when a volume extras page or official profile prints one.
