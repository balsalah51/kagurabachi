A sudden illness. An official recovery note. Issue 44, 28 September.

The September rest has a second door on this shelf. It keeps the outline, the lines already printed on the room, and the neighboring files.

Outline

  • Two rests in one year

Already on the page

The official Kagurabachi account announced that serialization scheduled for Weekly Shōnen Jump 2026 issue 42/43 combined (on sale 14 September) would rest because of the author’s sudden illness. The same notice said Takeru Hokazono had already recovered. Continuation: issue 44, on sale 28 September 2026. That issue’s weekly word is now printed as Misaka (巳坂).

The first 2026 rest followed chapter 126, “Fire”: a announced month so the kiln could stay honest. Ironworks returned 23 August. This September skip is a health notice with a printed return date. File the summer month on the 2026 rest. Chapter 131, “Tipping Point,” already printed 6 September. Official reading: VIZ or MANGA Plus.
