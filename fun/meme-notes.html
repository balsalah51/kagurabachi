Already on the page

September 2023. Promo art: black coat, white type, three goldfish. English Twitter invented a career for it. “The new Big Three.” “Generational.” Irony so thick it became a distribution strategy. Chapter 1 was the most-viewed new title on MANGA Plus in its first week. By April 2024 the app had logged over 99 million page views. It beat Spy × Family, Dragon Ball Super, and Boruto on the global ranking. People were actually reading.

Jump 2023 #42, 19 September. First long work after a Tezuka one-shot already named Enten and a run of Giga / Jump shorts. VIZ and MANGA Plus ran the English simulpub the same week, which is why a hashtag in New York could become a first-week chart in every timezone the app serves. Chapter 1 is usually free forever on MANGA Plus. That is how the joke became readers. People did not have to find a scan. They had to tap a title.
