Already on the page

The Hishaku are ten elite criminal sorcerers. Flame-emblem tattoos. A shared fire-gate. They formed about four years before the main story with one plan for Shinuchi. Three years before the present, they raided the Rokuhira house, murdered Kunishige, and stole the six wartime Enchanted Blades. Enten stayed with the son. In the same campaign they killed Ibuki Misaka so Cloud Gouger’s Lifelong Contract would open. They are not a gang that happened to find swords. They are a method: open the knot, spend the steel, change the country.

They are allied with Korogumi yakuza and, fatally, with at least one Kamunabi head. Kasen leaked the smith’s address because he thought Enchanted Blades were a path to order. The Hishaku were the path he hired. Recaps that call Sojo Hishaku cannot explain Yura. Sojo is a customer. They handed him Cloud Gouger in early October. He used it to chase Char and the idea that he could fake Kunishige’s eyes. They listed Magatsumi at the 208th Rakuzaichi after Yura cracked the seal and left the ropes. They signed a private contract with Samura. They are the men who sold a sword, auctioned a masterpiece, and tried to kidnap a daughter so a father would flinch.
