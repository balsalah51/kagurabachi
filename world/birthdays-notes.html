The dates Jump has actually printed. Everyone else stays blank until a profile page says otherwise.

Birthdays has a second door on this shelf. It keeps the outline, the lines already printed on the room, and the neighboring files.

Outline

This room has no subheadings. The title and the lede are the whole shape.

Already on the page

Five birthdays sit on this site because five profiles carry them. Ages are the present-tense book: Chihiro 18, Shiba 39, Sojo 30 at death, Kunishige 37 at death. Char’s age is not on her file. Other named figures (Hakuri, Hiyuki, Samura, Iori, Yura, Chiaki) do not have published dates here. Merch calendars cite more; those stay marked on the profiles page until we have seen the badge.

Kunishige and Sojo sit a day apart in June. The book does not make a scene of it; the profile pages just say the dates. The full name list, dates or not, is the name register. Cast at a glance: who is who.
