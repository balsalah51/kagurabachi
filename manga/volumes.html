Volume summaries

Vol. 01 — Mission

Chihiro takes Enten into Tokyo after a sighting. Char, Madoka, Azami, and Sojo’s first shadow. Palette: Rusty ruby / asphalt red, white title, Kuro goldfish.

Vol. 02 — Enten vs. Cloud Gouger

Hospital, Anti-Cloud Gouger Forces, the duel that breaks Kuregumo. Palette: Night steel vs storm cyan; blood on black.

Vol. 03 — Knight of Darkness

Hiyuki arrives. The Sazanami estate. Enten is surrendered on purpose. Palette: Flame-orange against auction green-black.

Vol. 04 — Equal

Hakuri awakens Isou and Storehouse. Yura at the auction. Tenri and Datenseki. Palette: Two figures, storehouse dusk.

Vol. 05 — Fervent

Storehouse war. Magatsumi twitch. Chihiro joins the Kamunabi on his terms. Palette: Crowded jacket, Shinuchi heat.

Vol. 06 — Daybreak

Train, kabuki, Senkutsuji. Tobimune returns to a blind man. Palette: Yin-yang bearers, blood center.

Vol. 07 — Night Battle

The false deaths. Owl over Japan. Operation: Easy Does It. Palette: Night blue, sunglasses, hotel dark.

Vol. 08 — Dawn

Iori’s past. Malediction spoken aloud. Samura walks into the duel. Palette: Hotel silhouette under Owl.

Vol. 09 — Enten

Enten vs Tobimune. Kunishige’s brief, said cleanly. Samura opens his eyes. Palette: Single-figure Enten jacket.

Vol. 10 — The Swordsmen

Kasen’s leak. HQ infiltration. Yura starts spending Shinuchi at range. Palette: Four-man war ensemble.

Vol. 11 — Heroes

Yukisada, the vessel, the cell. The Sword Master stands up in Yura. Palette: Snow white, Aka & Kuro, Enten raised.

Volume 12 (4 September 2026, ISBN 978-4-08-885177-8) is expected to close Part 1 (chs. 106–115) and open the war book.
