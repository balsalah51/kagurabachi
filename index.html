Chihiro Rokuhira 六平 千鉱

The swordsmith’s son. Age 18. He grew up at his father’s workshop after the Seitei War, then survived the raid that killed Kunishige. He took Enten — forged with him as a teenager — and entered the underworld with Togo Shiba to recover the stolen blades.

Enten shows as water and three goldfish: Kuro, the black flying slash; Aka, which drinks an attack and spends it; Nishiki, the tricolor cloak. After the war’s real cost comes into view, the brief on the seventh sword shifts: Kunishige made it to end the other six, especially Magatsumi.

Featured quote

“I really intended to go with koi until the last minute, but when finalizing the storyboard, I realized that goldfish would be easier to symbolize the daily life between Chihiro and his father. Plus, it’s about the ratio of the body to the fins.”

Takeru Hokazono, on why Enten is goldfish, not carp

Did you know…

  • Enten is the seventh Enchanted Blade. Kunishige forged it after the war, with Chihiro, to confront the other six — especially Magatsumi.
  • English videos still call the fish koi. Hokazono threw carp out on the storyboard; the bowl and the fins won. Goldfish, not koi.
  • The Sword Master, Akemura Soga, is Chihiro’s maternal uncle.
  • Chihiro is 18 (birthday 11 August). The anime casts Taihi Kimura; the voiced comic used Shoya Ishige.
  • After Sojo died, Chihiro briefly contracted the dying Cloud Gouger and spent its last charges at the Rakuzaichi.
  • The serial opened in Jump on 19 September 2023. Circulation passed 4 million by April 2026.
