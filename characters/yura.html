Overview

Yura is calm enough to bet his life on a coin, convinced the result is already blessed. He raided the Rokuhira house, sold Cloud Gouger to Sojo, put Magatsumi on the Rakuzaichi block after cracking its seal, and signed a private contract with Samura. He wanted the Sword Master dead so the Lifelong Contract would open. Then he spoke to Akemura and changed the math.

He is the Hishaku as a mind: not the loudest killer in the set of ten, the one who decides which blade goes to which monster.

Personality

Calm enough to bet his life on a coin, convinced the result is already blessed. That is not swagger. It is a man who treats fate as a colleague. He planned Kunishige’s murder so he could one day hold Magatsumi. He is Chihiro’s named target. He is also the only Hishaku who will change the math after a conversation. He wanted the Sword Master dead so the Lifelong Contract would open. Then he spoke to Akemura and recognized a colleague.

He is not the loudest killer in the set of ten. Hiruhiko is the scene. Hokuto killed Ibuki Misaka. Yura decides which blade goes to which monster. Cloud Gouger to Sojo in early October. Magatsumi on the Rakuzaichi block after the seal cracked. A private contract with Samura that looks like traitor-work. He is the collector Sojo would have become if Sojo had patience. The flame-emblem tattoo and the fire-gate are the letterhead. The mind is the product.

The conversion is the scary political joke. He wanted a corpse and a contract. He met a lucid patriot and offered a body. Chihiro’s generation inherits both the flowers and the lie about who planted them. Yura is the man who watered the lie for three years and then decided the gardener was worth joining.

Abilities

He is the leader of ten. Flame-emblem tattoo; fire-gate teleportation shared with the rest of the Hishaku. Named among the ten: Yura, Hokuto, Uran, Bingo, Yukisada, Hiruhiko, Kuguri, Toto. Allied with Korogumi yakuza and at least one Kamunabi head. The kit is logistics and assignment, not a named Enchanted Blade of his own, until Magatsumi.

He cracks Shinuchi’s seal, lists it at the 208th Rakuzaichi, then spends Magatsumi without drawing it. Yukisada sits in the barrier. The possession path is Magatsumi’s unique clause: anyone who holds the sheathed blade starts becoming Akemura. Kyora already proved it while dying. Yura walks into the cell and finishes the thought. He wanted to wield the Shinuchi. He later wants an alliance with its true bearer. Those are not the same goal. He is willing to lose the first to get the second.

The coin-bet is the personality made into a mechanic: convinced the result is already blessed. He is dangerous because he will spend a customer, a bearer, a hotel, and finally himself. Chihiro wounds him. He allows Akemura to possess him. The Sword Master stands up in the Hishaku lead. Volume 11 is that jacket.

Story role

Four years ago the Hishaku form: ten sorcerers, one plan for Shinuchi. Three years ago they raid the Rokuhira house. Kunishige dies. Six blades stolen. Ibuki Misaka murdered. Remaining bearers locked in Sanso. Yura ordered the raid. A Kamunabi director, Kasen, leaked the address. That is the wound chapter 1 opens on. Chihiro spends Vs. Sojo and the Rakuzaichi getting close enough to hear the name said out loud.

He sells Cloud Gouger to Sojo, then watches the customer die on Datenseki. He puts Magatsumi on the auction block. He is on the Rakuzaichi floor in Volume 4’s stretch. He signs the private contract with Samura: kill the other bearers, return the blades, kill the Sword Master, then die with the Hishaku. Samura uses that contract as cover for a Suzaku mercy. Yura still gets a temple that looks like a win.

The Sword Bearer Assassination arc is his endgame. Hiruhiko on the train and at the hotel. Kuguri in the house style Chihiro copies. Owl over the country. In Tokyo, Kasen’s leak is on the table, Kudo dies for Hakuri, Yura spends Magatsumi at range, Yukisada sits in the barrier. Shiba dumps the fight onto the street. Chihiro and Samura meet him. Volume 10’s jacket is the four-man war ensemble: Natsuki, Hokuto, Uruha, Yura. He loses. He gives the body to Akemura. The Master is loose in the Kamunabi. That is the last assignment he makes as himself.

Relationships

Chihiro is the named hunter. The panel where Yura tells him he orchestrated Kunishige’s murder is the revenge question asked in the room. Kunishige is the murder that was supposed to open a collection. Akemura is the colleague he did not expect to recognize, the conversion from “kill the Master” to “offer the body.”

Samura is the private contract: guilt over the Malediction put a Buddhist in a room with the Hishaku lead. Sojo is a customer, not a colleague. Hiruhiko is the younger blade sent to make a scene. Hokuto killed Cloud Gouger’s war bearer. Kasen is the Kamunabi head who leaked the address. Kyora is the auctioneer who listed Shinuchi and then touched it. Yura assigns; other people bleed.
