A poll before Cypic. Seventh is still a printed rank.

Before the studio had a name

Comic Natalie and Anime News Network recorded the 2024 AnimeJapan “manga you want adapted” ranking in March 2024. Hami Nakasora’s Shinanonchi no Ikuru took first. Kagurabachi placed seventh. The Cypic series was still two years from confirmation.

The poll is a want. The series is April 2027. Do not treat seventh place as a production order. Anime room: anime.
