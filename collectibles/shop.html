Buy the book. Buy licensed merch. Bootlegs, scans, and replica Enchanted Blades from a marketplace without a license line stay off this list.

The object to buy is the tankōbon. English volumes are VIZ. Japanese volumes are Jump Comics. Licensed hoodies, clocks, and keycaps live on official Jump storefronts. There is no public affiliate program for those Jump shops, so this site cannot take a cut of a Chihiro jacket. Amazon Associates is the honest US path for the VIZ spines. Crunchyroll Store’s old affiliate program ended 31 May 2026; those links are catalog only.

Commission, plainly. Amazon search links on this page pick up a tag if the site owner pastes an Associates ID into js/site.js as KAGURA.amazonTag (example shape: kagurabachi-20). Until that string is filled, the same buttons still open Amazon; nobody earns. Bookshop.org works the same way with KAGURA.bookshopId. Privacy already discloses the Amazon Associates line. No Associates tag is printed until one is pasted.
