How to be in the fandom

Sunday ritual日曜の儀式

The English timeline still lives under one hashtag. The legal way to be there is shorter than the leak way.

Jump drops. VIZ and MANGA Plus put the chapter up. Someone posts a still of a goldfish or an island. Someone else posts a 40-minute live reaction. Weekend Weebs writes 2,000 words before Monday lunch. That is the actual culture. Not a Discord of scanlations this archive will not link.

Official doors

  • VIZ Shonen Jump — English simulpub, app or web.
  • MANGA Plus — same week, Shueisha’s global app. Chapter 1 is usually free forever; that is how the meme became readers.
  • Weekly Shōnen Jump (JP)
  • X: @kb_anime_en · @VIZMedia · #Kagurabachi · #カグラバチ

Breaks happen. After chapter 126 the book took a announced month. That is a feature of weekly serialization, not a betrayal. Hokazono is one person with a swordsmith’s homework and a war flashback.

This site updates essays and the chapter index. It will not mirror unofficial pages, raws, or “early” Twitter dumps. If a video is built on a leak thumbnail, it does not get a card on the theory desk.
