Already on the page

An Enchanted Blade (妖刀, yōtō) overfills the body with spirit energy and leaves as a shape the bearer can steer: goldfish, clouds, flowers, feathers. Most have three named techniques and a True Realm when intent locks with the steel. Lifelong Contracts bind a blade to one nervous system and shut innate sorcery off. Magatsumi refuses the three-count. If Akemura dies, the other five wartime bearers die with him.

The public knows six. The Kamunabi want six. Enten is the seventh, forged after the war, because Kunishige could not smash the wartime steel on an anvil. Individual technique files live under Enchanted Blades. This page is the pictured introduction that used to sit on the homepage.
