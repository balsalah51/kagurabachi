The 70th list

Anime News Network recorded the 70th Shogakukan Manga Award nominees on 5 December 2024. Kagurabachi was among twelve works named, with The Apothecary Diaries and others. Nominations are not a personality.

The print-category win from the same year is Next Manga. Later 2025 prizes sit on Kodansha and Eisner.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
