The act

Tafuku Mihara is Hiyuki’s partner. Duel domain: two people, one match, then the street returns. Together they are how the Kamunabi wishes it looked. The Anti-Cloud Gouger Special Forces are how it looked in the first book: six specialists, four dead. Flame Bone is still one of the few innate arts the text will stand next to an Enchanted Blade.

The auction house is their first long room with Chihiro. The lesson is that the boy will spend a body without spending the people inside the building. Tafuku’s domain is how the bureau practices not burning the prefecture. Hiyuki’s skeleton is the license.

Where the act fails

Kasen’s leak and Akemura standing up in Yura make the perfect act insufficient. The HQ basement does not use referees. Magatsumi is not a match you schedule. The pair remains the state’s best public face. The face is not the leak. See duel domain and Flame Bone.
