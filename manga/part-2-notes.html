Already on the page

Part 1 runs chapters 1 through 115. “Swordsmith” (刀匠) closes the present tense: Enten in pieces, Samura dead, Akemura loose in the Kamunabi, Chihiro thinking like a smith again. Part 2 opens on chapter 116, “Princess” (姫), and does not stay for the press conference. It goes to Irishima. The Seitei War is not a clip show. It is the talks, the ore, the woman who can see, and the fire that turns a mineral into six national sins and, later, a seventh apology. This page is the Part 2 page for that book. The arc entry is the political frame. The chapter index is the title list. After chapter 126 the serial took an announced rest; 127-129 are the smelting continuing; 130, “I'm Fine!” (30 August 2026), pulls tamahagane from the furnace and puts Chiaki in the room. Volume 12 (4 September 2026, ISBN 978-4-08-885177-8) is expected to close Part 1’s last corridor and open this one on paper. A beginner door: Part 2, the forge door.

The title language already told you the present was a prologue. Early chapters are objects and meals. The long book names people, then puts quotation marks on the press’s words: “Strongest,” “Sword Master,” “Heroes.” Part 2 opens on a rank and a place. Princess. The Irishima Talks, four times, then END. Chiaki. Powerless. Smelting. Fire. Smelting again. Ironworks. The book is in the room where the sins are still a proposal. Chihiro has not been born. Enten does not exist. Shiba is still a Soga guardian. Mashiro is still alive. Kunishige is still a picky weapons dealer who barely eats because he will not sell to people he cannot stand.
