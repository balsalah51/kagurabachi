A fan-made, single-series reference. Kagurabachi only. No ads farm, no other Jump titles, no unofficial chapter hosts.

The two-site protocol

This archive is deliberately a hybrid of two English-language models that already work at scale for one franchise.

From Dragon Ball Wiki (Fandom): article pages with an infobox, technique lists, faction context, and related links. If you want to know who Hakuri is, what Storehouse does, and how he connects to Kyora, you should be able to do that in one scroll.

From Kanzenshuu: a curated manga guide — volume ISBNs, chapter titles, cover documentation, color-page notes, collectibles, and essays that treat the text as a publication, not only a plot dump. Kanzenshuu’s habit of describing jackets and color art in words (instead of pretending a scan is the work) is the house style for our cover studies.

Color

The site’s palette is taken from Hokazono’s own comment on the series look — white, black, and red for a story whose hero is stained by blood — and from Volume 1’s rusty ruby jacket plus Enten’s goldfish gold. Cloud Gouger pages pick up a cold cyan; Magatsumi pages go violet-black. Those are cover-adjacent accents, not a second theme.

Spoilers

Encyclopedia pages assume you have read through the current serialization. Late Part 1 and Part 2 revelations are marked with blacked-out spoiler text you can click to reveal. Arc summaries are written as after-action reports, not teaser copy.

What we do not host

Official manga pages, tankōbon scans, or unlicensed “manga pulls.” Color studies reconstruct jacket palettes from published descriptions. To read Kagurabachi, use VIZ or MANGA Plus. The work belongs to Takeru Hokazono and Shueisha.
