The series

Kagurabachi (カグラバチ) is a sword-sorcery serial by Takeru Hokazono. It opened in Weekly Shōnen Jump issue 2023 #42, on 19 September 2023 — his first long work after the Tezuka-winning one-shot Enten and a run of Jump Giga / Jump shorts. VIZ and MANGA Plus carry the English simulpub the same week. Jump Comics tankōbon started 2 February 2024 in Japan; VIZ’s English volumes started 5 November 2024.

The title is the book’s joke and its method. Kagura is shrine dance. Bachi is the strike. Hokazono draws a modern Japan that had to admit sorcery in public, then lie about what it did with it. The fighting language is a household object first: a bowl of goldfish, a katana in a cellar, a coat already stained black.

The premise

Kunishige Rokuhira is the only smith who ever stabilized Datenseki. During the Seitei War he cut six Enchanted Blades for the state. They reversed the front. After the treaty, his closest friend — Akemura Soga, the Sword Master, Chihiro’s uncle — used Magatsumi to empty the island anyway. About 200,000 civilians. The Kamunabi filed it as victory and hid the man.

Kunishige confiscated the six swords, hid, and raised his son over a cellar he could not smash. Fifteen years after the war they forged a seventh blade together: Enten. Three years before the main story, the Hishaku raided the house, killed Kunishige, and stole the wartime six. Chihiro kept Enten and went into the Tokyo underworld with Togo Shiba to take them back.

Chihiro is 18. Enten shows as water and three goldfish — Kuro, Aka, Nishiki — named for the bowl in the workshop. He is typically stoic. He will not spend innocent lives for the hunt. That line is the difference between him and almost every adult who touched the blades during the war.

Enchanted Blades

An Enchanted Blade (妖刀, yōtō) overfills the body with spirit energy and leaves as a shape the bearer can steer: goldfish, clouds, flowers, feathers. Most have three named techniques and a True Realm when the wielder’s intent locks with the steel. Lifelong Contracts bind a blade to one nervous system and shut the bearer’s innate sorcery off. Magatsumi refuses the three-count. If Akemura dies, the other five wartime bearers die with him.

  • Enten — seventh, post-war, Chihiro. Built to end the other six, especially Magatsumi.
  • Cloud Gouger (Kuregumo) — Ibuki → Sojo → Chihiro. Lightning, ice, weather. Bisected.
  • Magatsumi (Shinuchi) — Akemura. Flowers, insects, Malediction.
  • Kumeyuri — Uruha, later Hiruhiko. Hallucination and play.
  • Tobimune — Samura, then Iori. Feathers, owl, black fire.
  • Two wartime blades remain unnamed in the printed chapters. This site will not invent names.

The story so far

Part 1 (chs. 1–115) is three movements. Vs. Sojo (1–18) teaches the rules: contracts, Datenseki as a bomb, a government squad that is not enough, and a protagonist who will spend his body without spending Char. Rakuzaichi (19–46) is the 208th auction: Hakuri wakes the Storehouse, Kyora touches Shinuchi, Chihiro hands Magatsumi to the Kamunabi and keeps Enten by joining them. Sword Bearer Assassination (47–115) is the long book — Uruha, Samura, Iori, Kasen’s leak, Yura offering Akemura a body. Enten is bisected. Samura dies buying Chihiro a corridor. Part 1 ends on “Swordsmith.”

Part 2 (Seitei War, ch. 116–) returns to Irishima: Chiaki Soga, the talks, the ore, Kunishige before he was a hermit. The chapter index on this site runs through 126, “Fire.”
