Already on the page

An Enchanted Blade overfills. Spirit energy leaves the body as a shape that can be steered, seen through, and charged back into the steel so the bearer can spend techniques empty-handed. Most blades have three named techniques. Magatsumi refuses the three-count. The technique index is the same kit as a table. True Realm (Honryō) is not a transformation gauge. It is the brief, meant: the wielder’s intent locking with the steel until the kit grows an extension. Dark power (Kuroi chikara) is what happens when that lock happens at the edge of death, or when the blade itself is dying. The color goes black. The output jumps. Mei: Shred. Suzaku’s black flames. Magatsumi’s ordinary darkness, because Magatsumi eats vitality as a diet.

Unofficial names the fandom uses are marked with an asterisk where the pages themselves did not print a title. Full blade pages: Enchanted Blades. Contracts: Lifelong Contracts. The essay: True Realm.
