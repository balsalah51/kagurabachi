Author

Hokazono Takeru外薗 健

Born 6 September 2000, Osaka. Grew up on Jump. Started drawing during college lockdown. Then the goldfish ate the magazine.

He has said he wanted a Jump serial the way other kids want a sport. Naruto is the reason he tried; his editor has said it out loud, and Weekend Weebs logged the quote when people started the “is Flame Bone just Susanoo” thread. Early one-shots show it. The serial shows it less, except when a support blade sprouts an owl over Japan.

Before the weekly

  • Enten (炎天) — 100th Tezuka Award, printed in Jump Giga Spring 2021. Different story, same hunger for a named sword.
  • Farewell! Cherry Boy! — same Giga issue. The title is the joke.
  • Chain — Giga Summer 2021.
  • Madogiwa de Amu (Knitting by the Window) — Weekly Shōnen Jump 2022 #19.
  • Roku no Meiyaku — Jump 2022 #36/37. The one people point at when they say “he already knew how to end a one-shot on a debt.”

Kagurabachi starts in Jump 2023 #42 (19 September). First long work. Highest-voted title in a Manga Plus AX poll over Sakamoto Days, RuriDragon, and Blue Box.

What he cites

Anime NYC 2025: Katsuhiro Ōtomo books in the office as reference. Western action — he has named Captain America: The Winter Soldier as a Kagurabachi influence, and the Asahi interview adds the Hollywood-Japan crush (John Wick streets, Tarantino / Fincher / Nolan as the directors he grew up excited by). He has also been placed, by interviewers and by his own lists, near Chainsaw Man, Ajin, Attack on Titan, and My Hero Academia. The useful reading is not “he stole X.” It is that he likes movies that cut, then sit in the quiet.

He talked to a real swordsmith so the workshop pages would not be cosplay. Part 2’s smelting chapters are that homework coming due.
