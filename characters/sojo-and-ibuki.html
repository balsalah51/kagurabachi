One retired the sword. One paid for the leftover weather.

The opened contract

Ibuki Misaka was Samura’s equal in the Seitei War, the original Cloud Gouger bearer. He put the sword down afterward. Hokuto murdered him so the Lifelong Contract would open. Yura sold the weather sword to Sojo in early October. The customer arrives after the grave.

Sojo found cloaked Mei. He did not invent weather. Natsuki, the younger Misaka, kept training and holds Lightning Menace, voltage in the body, not Mei. Volume 10 puts Natsuki on a jacket with Hokuto. Sojo is already dead by then.

How recaps fail

Recaps that start Cloud Gouger with Sojo skip a wartime master and a Hishaku method. This desk files the customer on Volume 2 and the original on the brothers page. Chapter 132 is titled Misaka (巳坂). This desk still does not recap those panels. See Ibuki, the brothers, and Hokuto.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
