Already on the page

A typical Weekly Shōnen Jump color opening is a full-bleed illustration plus a typeset logo, used when a series is leading the issue or celebrating a volume. Kagurabachi’s color pages lean on the same triad as the jackets: Chihiro’s black coat, a red field or blood geometry, and a goldfish that is either Kuro-black or Aka-red. Snow and gold show up when Enten is being iconic rather than narrative (Volume 11; the 2026 anime teaser).

Chapter 1’s opening is the workshop and the raid in the same breath, the bowl and the Hishaku already sharing a color page. Chapter 8 is Sojo’s book opening in color before the duel has a name. Chapter 9 is the first Enten vs Cloud Gouger page, the one the Sojo essay calls two readers in one panel. Chapter 14 is True Realm entering the vocabulary. Chapter 18 is “Roar,” Kuregumo ending in the palette the crater earned. Chapter 19 lists Shinuchi. Chapter 23 is Hiyuki on the auction pages. Chapter 44 is the curtain. Chapter 47 opens the long book on Uruha’s train. Chapter 60 is the elevator English videos still pause. Chapter 83 is Enten’s brief. Chapter 113 is the island.
