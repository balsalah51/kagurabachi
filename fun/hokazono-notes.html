Already on the page

He has said he wanted a Jump serial the way other kids want a sport. Naruto is the reason he tried; his editor has said it out loud, and Weekend Weebs logged the quote when people started the “is Flame Bone just Susanoo” thread. Early one-shots show it. The serial shows it less, except when a support blade sprouts an owl over Japan.

He grew up on Jump. He started drawing during college lockdown. The Tezuka Enten is not Kagurabachi’s seventh blade, different kanji, different story, but it is the same hunger for a named sword in a magazine that still sells named swords. Roku no Meiyaku is the short that already knew how to end on a debt. Two years later the weekly would end Part 1 on “Swordsmith.”
