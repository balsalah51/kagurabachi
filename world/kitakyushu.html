What is printed

Ibuki was ferocious in his younger years and often fought in his hometown of Kitakyushu alongside his younger brother Natsuki. That sentence is why this desk has a place room. The brothers’ combined skill is the wartime rumor. Lightning Menace stays Natsuki’s innate art. Cloud Gouger is the contract only one of them received.

Ibuki’s actions put him at odds with the Sorcery Bureau. After the war he abandoned swordsmanship. Hokuto killed the retired man. Chapter 132’s title is the house that city already knew. We do not invent a street map. See the brothers and the house.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
