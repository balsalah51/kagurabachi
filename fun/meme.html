Reception

Meme to flagshipネタから本誌へ

People decided a guy with a sword was the next Big Three before page one existed. Then the book had to survive being liked for real.

September 2023. Promo art: black coat, white type, a fish that was not a Dragon Ball aura. English Twitter invented a career for it. “The new Big Three.” “Generational.” Irony so thick it became a distribution strategy. Chapter 1 was the most-viewed new title on MANGA Plus in its first week. By April 2024 the app had logged over 99 million page views. It beat Spy × Family, Dragon Ball Super, and Boruto on the global ranking. That is not a meme metric. That is people actually reading.

Hokazono walked into an Anime NYC room that was not billed as a main event and found it stuffed. ANN’s write-up is the useful one: the joke fandom became a real one because the pages were good, not because the hashtag was loud. Next Manga Award, print category, 2024. Nominations later for Shogakukan, Kodansha, and an Eisner international slot. Circulation: 1 million by October 2024, 2.2 million by May 2025, 3 million by October 2025, 4 million by April 2026.

The archive’s position is unromantic. Ironic hype got the first chapter opened. The Vs. Sojo fight, Hakuri, and Samura are why people stayed. If you are sending the book to someone who only knows the meme, send the first-read notes and chapter 1 on MANGA Plus, not a “generational” compilation.

The word bachi in the title is still a Sunday in-joke — punishment, a strike, a sound. Official anime tags it #BachiAnime. We do not pretend that was the marketing plan in 2023.
