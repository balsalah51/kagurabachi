Purpose

Kunishige could not smash the wartime blades. Enten is the tool he built instead: a sword whose True Realm is the death of Magatsumi. Nishiki’s cloak shrugging off Spider and vitality-drain is not a random buff. It is the design brief.

The public knows six Enchanted Blades. The Kamunabi want six. Chihiro’s early swagger, the seventh sword, the secret cellar, reads as inheritance. Chapter 83 and the Kyoto duel with Samura retract that reading. Enten exists because the other swords would not break on an anvil. It is the only blade whose success condition is fewer blades. See What Enten was forged for.

History

After the Seitei War, Kunishige confiscated the six wartime swords, hid with help from Azami and Shiba, and raised Chihiro over a cellar he could not smash. Every attempt to destroy them failed. Nearly fifteen years after the war they forged Enten together, the first blade built as a counter rather than a weapon of state.

Three years before the main story the Hishaku raided the house, killed Kunishige, and took the wartime six. Enten stayed with the son. Chihiro took it into the Tokyo underworld, through Vs. Sojo and the Rakuzaichi, then into the Kamunabi on his own terms: Magatsumi to the state, Enten still in his hand.

Techniques

Enten leaves the body as water and three goldfish. Spirit energy can be steered, seen through, and charged back into the steel so Chihiro can spend techniques empty-handed. The fish are named for the bowl in the Rokuhira house.

Kuro 涅

The black fish. It paints arm and edge in black water and throws a cut the size of the fish you called. Kuro: Shred spends more spirit to throw many small ones. It is the workhorse, the first language Chihiro speaks in a fight.

Aka 猩

The red fish. It drinks a technique and lets you speak it back for a short window. Absorb, then return. Useful against other Enchanted Blades, and against anyone who thinks a named art is a one-way street.

Nishiki 錦

The tricolor cloak. Speed, density, and a built-in resistance to other Enchanted Blade ailments. That last clause is Enten’s purpose showing through the technique list: a support kit aimed at Magatsumi’s field, not a fashion layer.

True Realm

Honryō. When the wielder’s intent locks with the steel. Enten’s True Realm is Magatsumi’s death. Chihiro reaches it first against Sojo, not because he is louder, because he means the cut. True Realm is not a Super Saiyan form. It is the brief, said with the whole nervous system. See Sorcery.

Craft

Hokazono almost used koi. Goldfish won because the fin-to-body ratio read better on the page and because the bowl recorded the small contest of who the fish liked more. A wartime blade manifests weather, flowers, banquet ghosts. Enten manifests the house. The fighting language is a household object first.

“In a revenge tale where the protagonist’s clothes are stained black with blood, the colors white, black, and red match well.”
Takeru Hokazono, on why the fish, and the palette, are not koi
“I really intended to go with koi until the last minute, but when finalizing the storyboard, I realized that goldfish would be easier to symbolize the daily life between Chihiro and his father. Plus, it’s about the ratio of the body to the fins. With koi, the body is long, and even if you draw the fins beautifully, they end up looking chubby.”
Takeru Hokazono, on Enten

The long fish looked like a weapon. The bowl fish looked like a life. Full interview notes live on Goldfish, not koi.

Status

Chihiro is the only bearer. He briefly contracted the dying Cloud Gouger as well and spent its last charges at the Rakuzaichi; Enten remained the partner. At the end of Part 1 Magatsumi bisects Enten. Samura’s Suzaku-black stalls the rot. Chihiro keeps the pieces and starts notes toward a new Enten, and even a new Cloud Gouger, like a smith again.

The catalog version

Kuro, Kuro: Shred, Aka, Nishiki, Nishiki: Support. True Realm: Magatsumi’s death. Dark power is not Enten’s usual color; the goldfish stay a household even when the coat is black. The full list with the other four named blades sits on the technique catalog. Part 2’s kiln is why this seventh sword will have to exist.
