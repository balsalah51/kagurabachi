Already on the page

Sanso are Kamunabi fortresses assigned to surviving wartime Enchanted Blade bearers after the Rokuhira raid and Ibuki’s murder. The logic is the Lifelong Contract: while the bearer lives, the Hishaku cannot fully sign the stolen steel. Lock the person, stall the ten. It works until it does not. Kokugoku is attacked. Senkutsuji is attacked. Subaru is relocated. The long book of Part 1, Sword Bearer Assassination, is the Sanso system meeting a method that had three years of practice. This page is the buildings. The Kamunabi page is the bureau that staffed them. The Hishaku page is the method.

Uruha’s Sanso. The Kokugoku Steam Squad, including Fushimi and Smoke Axe, are the guard. Hishaku-hired Datenseki troops hit first. The squad beats them. Then Hiruhiko arrives. Blood Crane, mercenary sorcerers, a train, a kabuki house. The Steam Squad dies. Chihiro fights Hiruhiko so Hakuri can move Uruha toward Senkutsuji. Chapter 48 is titled for the squad. They get a name and a grave in the same week. That is how the bureau spends specialists: named, competent, insufficient. The Anti-Cloud Gouger Special Forces were the same sentence in the first book.
