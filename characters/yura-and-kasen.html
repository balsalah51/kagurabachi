Yura and Kasen幽と火煎

They are not partners in a speech bubble. They are why the workshop had a mailing address.

The leak

Kasen is a Kamunabi director who thinks order lives in Magatsumi. He leaks the Rokuhira address. Three Hishaku sorcerers hit the workshop. Kunishige dies. Six blades leave. Enten stays. Yura is the mind who ordered the raid. The leak is stationery. The raid is the letter.

Azami and Kudo spent capital the other way. Hiyuki is the pointed end that still has to live in Kasen’s bureau. Chihiro’s deal, Magatsumi to the state, assumes a director who wants blades sealed. Kasen wanted them used. Those are not the same Kamunabi.

The cell

Yura walks to the cell and offers a body. Akemura leaves in that offer. Kasen’s order and Yura’s offer meet in the same basement. The building that hid the Sword Master loses him. See Kasen and the leak essay.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
