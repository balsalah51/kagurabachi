A seventh blade built as a retraction. An uncle who kept the masterpiece.

Kinship the press never sold

Wikipedia’s war file and this archive’s volume 12 rooms already name Akemura as Chihiro’s maternal uncle. Magatsumi is the Shinuchi. Enten’s True Realm is that blade’s death. The raid made a revenge plot. The uncle makes the revenge a family argument.

We do not recap later uncollected panels here. The printed pair is enough: Chiaki’s brother, Kunishige’s in-law, the man the quoted press called Sword Master. See Akemura, Magatsumi, the seventh as apology.

Official chapters live on VIZ or MANGA Plus. This desk does not host pages.
